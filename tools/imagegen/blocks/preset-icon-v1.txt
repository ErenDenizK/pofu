PRESET ICON: a painted icon or a family of icons. Fill the fields of the brief in this order: PURPOSE (what each icon means), AUDIENCE, SUBJECT (one object per icon), LAYOUT (a family sits on an even grid with equal size and spacing, nothing touching), STYLE (the attached icons and painting, the same finish for every icon of the family), PALETTE (milky pastel with one warm-brown accent shape per icon), CONSTRAINTS. Each icon is one centered object of three or four large rounded shapes that reads as a silhouette at 24 points; no thin parts, no outline, no background shape, no text, no letters, no numbers, no glass, no glossy highlight.
