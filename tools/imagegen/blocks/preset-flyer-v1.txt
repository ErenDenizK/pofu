PRESET FLYER: a portrait flyer with a picture and a short message. Fill the fields of the brief in this order: PURPOSE, AUDIENCE, TEXT HIERARCHY (headline, subline, one line of detail), REQUIRED TEXT (every line spelled exactly, letter for letter, with every accent mark, and paint no other words), LAYOUT (the picture in the upper two thirds, the text block below on a calm pale area), STYLE (the attached painting), PALETTE (pastel). Painted text is large, rounded, hand-lettered, dark warm brown, fully legible. No border, no QR code, no glass, no buttons, no logos of other companies.
