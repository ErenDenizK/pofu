STYLE: soft painterly 2D illustration from the same picture book as the
attached hamster: the same gentle airbrushed shading, rounded chunky
shapes with few parts, matte materials with broad smooth value changes,
no outlines, no hard edges. LIGHT AND ATMOSPHERE ARE THIS PLACE'S OWN,
not the home island's: the sky, the hour, the direction, colour and
hardness of the key light, the colour of the shadows, the haze, the
weather and what lies below the island are exactly as the brief states,
and they repaint the whole frame, edge to edge, clouds included. Do not
lift everything toward cream or pastel; use the full value range the
brief asks for, with a clear lightest light and a clear darkest dark
(deep, never pure black). One warm light somewhere in the frame, small.
Keep it cozy: wonder, not danger. No text, no letters, no numbers, no
logos, no watermark, no border, no frame, no sticker outline.
