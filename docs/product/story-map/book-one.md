# Book One story map: "Bekçi kim?"

**Status:** canonical for Book One (lead proposal 2026-11-05, shown to the
founder 2026-11-05 and 2026-11-06; supersedes conflicting docs). Where any
other doc disagrees on Book One's islands, order, residents, themes, Söz
lines, gifts or Nazlı's growth, this file wins and that doc is fixed.

The founder saw these two pages on 2026-11-05; they were not committed, the
tracker then called them "never made" and new docs drifted from them. They
are committed here as shown:

- [Page 1](book-one-p1.png): premise, units, the iceberg, Söz, Ayçiçeği,
  Gezgin, Çağlayan, Revontuli.
- [Page 2](book-one-p2.png): Olta, Emanet, Mehtap and the finale, the
  Book Two spark.

The pictures on the pages are chosen concept tries, not promoted art
("görseller: seçilmiş konsept denemeleri (terfi edilmemiş)"). Sources named
on the page: story-references, deep-lore, home-layout §10, founder-input

Turkish is quoted verbatim from the pages; English around it is a
translation or a structural note.

## 1. Premise

> **Bekçi kim?**
>
> "Dört ana ada, bir balina, bir final gecesi. **Ada biter, yolculuk
> bağlar.** Her ana ada kendi küçük hikâyesini kapatır; altında Gezgin,
> ikili ve taşlar yolculuğu taşır. Final: iz evde biter ve **Bekçi
> sensin**."

Four main islands, one whale, one finale night. Each island closes its own
small story; underneath, Gezgin, the duo and the stones carry the journey.
The finale: the trail ends at home, and the keeper is you.

### 1.2 Söz, Kitap 1

"On iki satırın dördü; sadece Mochi okur, çayla." Four of the twelve lines;
only Mochi reads them, with tea. "**1** Çağlayan · **4** Revontuli · **7**
Emanet · **10** Mehtap". "Yan yana okununca: bir bekçinin işi, ve eve giden
yol." Read together: a keeper's work, and the way home.

| Line | Island | Where it is found | The line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Çağlayan | Mochi pours tea on the mill stone | *"Birinin uykusu için ışığı söndüren, o gece bekçidir."* |

### 1.3 Hiç değişmez (never changes)

"Tohum ekran süresi satın almaz. Pofu asla ölmez. Uçurum yok: kitap 'iyi
geceler' ile biter." Seeds never buy screen time; Pofu never dies; no
cliffhanger, the book ends on "good night" (ADR-0015, ADR-0017).

  Damla, Çarşamba Emanet's parcel (Boncuk), Cuma Tuli, Cumartesi Arı's

  first visitor) → Çağlayan → Çanlar, Martı Kayası (random) → Revontuli →
  Olta (midpoint) → Fanus, Pervane → Emanet → Çömlek, Arı (home upgrade) →
  Mehtap and the finale → Yakamoz, Yosun's letter. Leylek any time, in real
  spring.
- **Games:** place (home), flow (Çağlayan; Revontuli adds light), recall
  (Çanlar's "dinle ve tekrarla"), a story island (Emanet). The page names
  no game for Mehtap.
- **The seventh filler** in "+ 7 filler" is not drawn. Open for the lead:
