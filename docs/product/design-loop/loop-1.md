## 0. The answer

- **The mystery, in two lines.** Somebody keeps switching off lights in
  the loud world below, one at a time, and every light switched off
  becomes a little quiet that lets an island come home. All of Book One,
  Pofu follows the trail of this night watchman, the **Bekçi**; at the
  real full moon the trail ends at his own island, tonight: **the Bekçi
  is the player.**
- **The duo is Pofu and Mochi.** Pofu (the Atreus) hears the islands and
  does not know what he is; Mochi (the Kratos), a lop bunny whose ears
  hang down so she cannot hear them, comes along "to bring the tea" and
  hides that she knew the last Bekçi. He learns he did not stop running
  alone; she learns to sit down and stop waiting for someone who left.
- **Çağlayan becomes the falls that remember.** Water that has gone round
  the sky shows what it passed: every pool you clear becomes a live window
  onto another island, a resident, or a rumour not yet arrived. It keeps
  that job forever, and its deepest pool shows the first rung of the
  mystery: a far window whose light goes out.
- **Constellations with controlled deltas.** Seven clusters, each a grade
  of the home palette plus one accent, bounded so every cluster stays kin
  to Ayçiçeği and none is green except home (section 3.1).
- **Pacing derived from content.** The tool derives every arrival day
  from the newest island's runway (its run of fresh days), a minimum gap
  between big wows, and the founder's targets. Result: Çağlayan d4, Koke
  d6, Gezgin d9, Çanlar d13, Martı d15, Revontuli d17 (rumour d14), then
  one arrival every 2–5 days to Mehtap on d41 and its full-moon bloom.
  **Zero dead days, zero stacked wows** (section 4).
- **Why Revontuli is a 10:** it is not an island on the sky, it **replaces
  the sky**: its own hour and weather, one cold temperature with one warm
  light, light that moves, water in two states, a real place's mood, and
  a small warm thing in a vast cold space. Gezgin sits at 7 because it is
  a great idea in a borrowed daylight. Every island gets the seven-part
  **Revontuli formula** as concrete upgrades (section 3.0); Gezgin
  becomes the night crossing.
- **Premium and god-tier, distributed as a sawtooth with a rising
  floor:** every big and parallel island is premium at least; god-tier
  islands are chapter climaxes (Gezgin the hook on d9, Revontuli d17,
  Emanet d30, Mehtap the finale), never two in a row, at least 7 days
  apart; premium islands between them carry the setup and the payoff;
  satellites keep the rhythm (section 4.1). Checked by the tool, which
  also ingests the founder's scores after every loop.
- **Ayçiçeği is the heart, not the first island:** it grows from a
  hamster's island into a small harbor town with a square (meydan) where
  every island leaves a building, residents lodge, and the year's feasts
  and its finale happen (section 3.4).
- **The backdrop** turns the founder's favourite concept image into a
  system: home in front, constellations receding in four depth layers
  with haze, satellites orbiting, everything graded by the hour
  (section 5). One far-view painting and one emissive mask per island.
- **A concept round of 12 images** tests the risky ideas before any
  production (section 6).

## 2. The big story: Book One

### 2.1 The two layers

**Above the waterline (what the game looks like for weeks):** a hamster,
his island, a tea stall, and wonder islands with funny residents whose
small stories end. Each island's surface story is complete and cozy on its
own. Nothing asks the player to solve anything.

**Below the waterline (writers only; never put in the game):**

- Every light switched off in the loud world below drifts up as a little
  quiet. Quiet settles the sky's currents; islands that can hear each
  other drift together (lore-bible §1 stands).
- The sky remembers each switched-off light three ways: as **clear water**
  (Çağlayan's pools show it), as **aurora** (Revontuli), and as a **dark
  star**, a gap in the sky where a light was (Mehtap's chart).
- Whoever switches a light off for someone's quiet is, that night, a
  **Bekçi**: the night watchman of a Turkish neighbourhood, who walks at
  night so that others sleep. Ayçiçeği had one before Pofu: the one who
  wrote the practical letters (lore M4) and the four notes. When a new
  Bekçi came, the old one rode south with the storks.
- **Pofu's origin, revised.** Pofu ran a wheel beside a bright window that
  never went dark. On the player's day one, it went dark: not a battery,
  a hand. He stopped to hear better. The bright window is the player's
  own screen; this is never shown or said, but every clue is built from
  it: the night-light on Emanet's shelf is tagged with **the player's real
  day-one date**, Fanus's tags are **the player's real quiet days**,
  Revontuli's aurora is brighter after a calm night (all deterministic,
  on-device, from logs the app already keeps).
- **The new Bekçi is the player.** Every resist switches off one light.

The story's thesis, never said: _you are the one who made it quiet, and
someone is glad._ It is the opposite of a guilt app: the player is the
hero of a world they never saw themselves in.

### 2.2 The duo: Pofu and Mochi

**Why a duo now.** Story-delivery §3 recommended Pofu alone. The founder
asked for a travelling pair that talks, like Kratos and Atreus. A second
voice is safe if it speaks **only inside duo exchanges** that the player
triggers (section 2.5), never on the gate.

**Who travels with Pofu.** Four candidates:

| Candidate | For | Against | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mochi**, lop bunny, çay ocağı | Lop ears hang down, so she **cannot hear the islands**: the perfect foil to a hamster who can. Her want (everyone stays for a second glass) and need (to sit down) are what a ferry gives: on a vapur you can only sit, and Turkish ferries serve tea. She already has a hidden past (the crowded tea garden) that can hold the Kratos secret. The aunt and the little brother (teyze and yeğen) is a dynamic every Turkish player knows. Already painted | The çay ocağı needs someone when she travels | **Chosen.** Simit minds the stall in the afternoons, which is exactly Simit's arc (learning to spend the afternoon) |
| Fındık, hedgehog | A good gruff Kratos | His need is to leave things as they are; travel turns him into a fixer of every island, the opposite of the theme | Stays home; mends what the duo brings back |
| Simit, kitten | Energy, comedy | Two young voices make no tension; her arc is the user's, and it happens at home | Minds the stall |
| The moth (Pervane) | Cannot nag | Cannot talk; not a duo | Stays the wordless compass |

**The mapping (as a writer's tool, not a copy):**

| God of War (2018) | Pofu |
|---|---|
| Kratos: old, guarded, protective, hides a past, terse | **Mochi**: older, practical, terse ("açık mı, demli mi?"), hides that she knew the old Bekçi; keeps his glass upside down on the top shelf |

Rules: one open question at a time; a rung is **an object or an image
first** and at most one line; nobody explains a rung; nothing is said
about phones (lore §6.11).

### 2.4 Momentum without breaking the cozy tone

- **Escalate scale and closeness, never danger.** The questions grow: a
  pool → a letter → a song → the whole sky → a trail → you. Stakes are
  understanding, not survival. Nothing chases, nothing is lost.
- **Every step is big because it changes three things you can see:** the
  island blooms, home gains a gift, and the **backdrop** changes (a new
  silhouette comes forward; section 5).
- **Callbacks make the past grow.** Late secrets on old islands (the wheel
  in Çağlayan's falls, Zil's bell in Koke's tree, the whale moored at the
  falls) fire during Mehtap's wait, when the story is at its biggest.
- **Endings are quiet and complete** (lore §6.8). The reveal is a look, a
  glass and one line, then "İyi geceler".

### 2.5 How the duo speaks (delivery)

- **Where:** the vapur ride (the travel transition to an island, first
  visit of the day), arrivals, stage turns (S3), blooms and feasts. Never
  on the shield, Hop!, widgets or notifications.
- **Budget:** at most **one duo exchange a day**, two to four lines, always
  skippable, kept in the Seyir defteri. Mochi never speaks outside an
  exchange. The "Az konuşsun" setting also silences duo exchanges except
  arrivals and blooms.
- **Writing:** IDs `DUO-xx` and `<ISLAND>-Sx` with intent and subtext, as
  above; the founder and friends write the words; the `pofu-story` skill's
  device rule applies (no two consecutive exchanges on the same device).

## 3. Islands and clusters

### 3.0 Why Revontuli is a 10, and the formula every island takes

The founder's question: "why is it this good?" Looking at the painting
(`batch-10-island-concepts-pilot/x-revontuli-1`) beside Gezgin and the
rest, seven things Revontuli does that the others do not, in order of
weight:

| # | What Revontuli does | Why it lands | Who lacks it |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **It replaces the whole sky.** Its own hour (night) and weather (snow) repaint every pixel, not only the island | Visiting it feels like travelling; the frame itself changes temperature | Gezgin, Çanlar, Koke, Çömlek, Emanet sit under home's pastel daylight |
| R2 | **One temperature, one warm light.** Cold blue-green everywhere, and one honey window in the hut | Complementary contrast is the oldest trick of mood painting; the warm light is home's honey, so it feels kin | Most islands are evenly warm and evenly lit |
| R3 | **Light that moves.** The aurora is light, not an object; it animates the whole sky | Motion without clutter; depth from emissive layers | Islands whose motion is a prop (a mill, bunting) |
| R4 | **A material you can almost touch, in two states.** Snow and ice, dark water in a round hole | Texture is felt; the hole promises something under | Lawns, generic stone |
| R5 | **A simple silhouette.** A floe, spruces, a hut | Reads at 60 pt; the sky does the work | Emanet's clutter, Çömlek's postcard |
| R6 | **A real place's mood.** A Nordic winter night, the sauna, revontulet | Specific beats pretty; it is from somewhere | "Sky-native" islands with no root |
| R7 | **Small warmth in a vast cold.** One fox, one hut, a huge sky | Coziness is contrast: warm inside, wide outside | Islands that fill the frame with things |

**Why Gezgin is a 7.** The idea is a 10 (an island that is alive), the
painting is a 7: daylight borrowed from home (R1 missing), evenly warm
(R2), the motion is the whale's slow body only (R3 weak), the back is a
meadow and a cottage, i.e. the home template on a whale (R4, R5), and no
mood of its own (R6). **Upgrade: the night crossing.** Gezgin travels at
dusk between fog banks like an evening ferry; the cloud sea darkens to
periwinkle; along her flank, **bioluminescent freckles glow like a
ferry's portholes** (R2, R3); on her back not a lawn but a **barnacle
village** of round cloud-barnacle houses with warm windows, Yosun's post
office sheltered under the blowhole (R4, R5); when she breathes, her
spout falls as a **local shower with a moonbow** (R1, R3, her own
weather); the duo sits on her back like ferry passengers with tea
(R6: the vapur crossing is the most Turkish mood there is; R7: tiny duo,
huge whale, wide dusk). Her breathing still sets the slide game's pace.

### 3.2 Çağlayan first: the falls that remember

**Mercilessly, what was still wrong after curation's rework.** "The fall
has stopped; a boat blocked the spring; it pours again" is the default
cozy arc (restore a broken thing), and it would be the first of ten
islands that heal. The pools were decoration. The island had no job once
finished. And its story ended where every island's ends: it got better.

**Ideas tried and rejected in this pass:**

| Idea | Why not |
|---|---|
| The fall pours upward | Striking once; reads as a bug at 60 pt; no verb grows out of it |
| An Escher loop (the fall feeds itself) | A famous image (Escher's _Waterfall_, 1961): derivative |
| A silent waterfall that mutes whatever it touches | Lovely line, but kills the arrival scene's "did you hear?" and the island's sound |
| Water that carries memories you collect | Collecting memories is a sad-app trope; collecting needs a counter |
| **Water that has gone round the sky shows what it passed** | **Chosen**: it gives the pools a verb, a forever job, and the first rung |

**The design.**

- **Silhouette:** the travertine column of curation (`x2-caglayan-b`): a
  white frozen fountain whose pools spiral down in rings, the fall
  pouring off the lowest rim into the clouds, longer than the island is
  tall. Palette: the water delta (chalk and teal, sunflower glints).
- **The unforgettable thing: the pools are windows.** Water that went
  round the sky (fell into the clouds, rained on other islands, came back
  up the spring) shows, for a while, what it passed. A cleared pool shows,
  live and deterministic: the day's awake island, a resident doing
  something right now, or the **silhouette of a rumoured island** before
  it arrives. A pool is a window, not a screen: it ripples, it holds
  weather, a leaf can drift across the image.
- **The mechanic, made juicy:** route the channels (the Flow grammar),
  but the reward of a solved board is visible on the island: a pool
  clears, and its picture fades in. Boats race through solved channels
  and splash into the pool. Stage 1 boards are four tiles.
- **The toy:** drop a leaf boat over the rim. Days later it bumps the
  home pier (unchanged), **and in between you can watch it in the pools,
  passing other islands.** The toy becomes a little travelogue.
- **Forever job in the web (why it never gets boring): Su aynası.** After
  stage 5 the top pool shows each morning's awake island and the next
  rumour's silhouette. It is the archipelago's window; Çanlar is its ear,
  Emanet its memory.
- **Arc.** Wound: the pools are milky; Damla says the water "forgot".
  Want (Damla): catch the whole fall in her tea glass, to keep the
  pictures. Need: let it go round; a picture lives only in moving water.
  Turn (S3): **the player's own leaf boat** jammed the spring, so the
  water could not go round (curation's beat, kept, now with a reason).
  S4: the mill turns "for no reason" (M2's rung). **Bloom (S5):** the
  fall at full, every pool clear at once, the whole sky in the island for
  the first time. In the deepest pool, for one breath, **a far window
  whose light goes out**. Pofu's ears flatten. Mochi sees water.
- **Feast:** rain on home (the water came round); Damla's full glass
  given, not drunk (lore §7.3 stays).
- **Gifts to home:** at arrival, the spring by the pond, whose surface
  shows Çağlayan's fall (a window at home); at S5, morning rainbows after
  calm nights.
- **What it changes in the big story:** rung 1 (a light went out, and Pofu
  knows the window). Re-read at Emanet: it was the player's day one.
- **Its satellite, Koke:** a bonsai pine riding a travertine boulder that
  orbits **through Çağlayan's spray**, so it rains on Koke only when the
  fall pours: Koke's rain grows with Çağlayan's stages. Sumi, the
  dormouse, rakes one closed line around the stones each night (the
  raked-loop game); the lines are thank-you letters "to whoever makes it
  quiet" (rung 2). No torii, no sakura, no Fuji (curation §3.5).

## 4. Pacing: days derived from content

### 4.1 Premium and god-tier: the sawtooth with a rising floor

The founder's question: every island must be meaningful, fun, innovative
and useful; if of about ten concepts five are premium and five god-tier,
how should they be spread while the bar keeps rising?

**Answer: a sawtooth with a rising floor.** Two god-tier islands in a row
flatten each other (the second wow is measured against the first and
loses; the player has no breath to feel either); a long stretch of
premium islands makes the bar feel like it stopped rising. So:

- **Every island is premium at least** (intensity ≥ 7 of 10; a big or
  parallel island the founder scores under 7 is redesigned or re-tiered,
  and the tool shortens its runway until then: a weak island holds the
  player less long).
- **God-tier islands are chapter climaxes**, one per chapter: the **hook**
  early (Gezgin, d9, inside the free-to-Plus decision window), a
  **mid-book peak** (Revontuli d17, Emanet d30), the **finale** last and
  highest (Mehtap). Never two in a row among big and parallel islands;
  at least 7 days apart.
- **Premium islands are the breathing room** that carry the setup for the
  next peak and the payoff of the last (Çağlayan sets up the whale's
  letters; Çanlar sets up the aurora's meaning; Fanus sets up Emanet's
  shelf; Çömlek closes Mochi's arc before the finale).
- **The floor rises:** the premium baseline never falls (8, 8, 8, 9), so
  week six's "ordinary" island is better than week one's.
- **Satellites and parallels keep the rhythm** between peaks; they are
  untiered, short, and must still pass the formula of section 3.0.

| Chapter | Days | Premium (setup) | Rhythm | God-tier (climax) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The window | 1–12 | Çağlayan (8) | Koke | **Gezgin (9), the hook** |
| 2. The listener | 13–24 | Çanlar (8) | Martı, Olta | **Revontuli (10)** |
| 3. The keeper | 25–32 | Fanus (8) | Pervane | **Emanet (9)** |
| 4. The Bekçi | 33–45+ | Çömlek (9) | Arı, Yakamoz | **Mehtap (10), the finale** |

With exactly five and five, the fifth god-tier island would open Book
Two (a hook again), never sit beside another peak. The intensities are
designed; the founder's scores replace them as he gives them (the loop's
input, [README](README.md)).

### 4.2 The schedule

Rules (`profiles.yaml` → `rules`): an island's **runway** is its run of
consecutive days with a fresh beat from arrival (about ten minutes of new
play or story a day); the next island arrives when the newest one's
runway ends, but never sooner than 2 days after any arrival, 5 days after
a big one, or within 3 days of another **wow** (a big arrival or a big
bloom). A **dead day** has no fresh beat from any island; a **thin day**
has only a daily (Çanlar's rumour, Emanet's puzzle); more than two thin
days in a row fails. Late secrets on old islands are beats.

satellites ≥ 16 pt).

### 5.3 Time of day and weather

- **Grade:** each far view is graded by the colour script's hour cell,
  then hazed toward the hour's sky colour by its layer's haze.
- **Rim light** from the sun's direction at golden hour (a gradient mask,
  code).
- **Night:** silhouettes darken, **emissive masks** win: Çağlayan's mist
  catches moonlight, Revontuli's aurora, Çanlar's bell lanterns, Emanet's
  lamps, Çömlek's kiln, Pervane's lantern, Mehtap itself. The far sky at
  night is a map of lights: the story's language (lights as presence).
- **Weather:** rain shortens the haze distance (L2 fades to 80 %), fog
  hides L2 except emissives, clear nights show everything.
- **Awake islands** (1A.4) get a small cue in the backdrop (a lit window,
  a bell glint, smoke), never a badge.

- Logic: pure and tested like `FarSkyLayout`: which islands are visible
  for a given game state (arrived, rumoured, newest cluster), and their
  placement at a rail position and wall-clock time.
- Budget: ≤ 20 sprites, one pass, far views at 2× displayed size.
- Order of work: (1) the concept round below decides the slot map;
  (2) far views for the first three clusters; (3) code.

## 7. Self-critique

- **"The Bekçi is the player" can tip into flattery or meta.** It works
  only if it is earned by real data (dates, calm nights) and said once,
  with a look and a glass. If the human line sounds like a reward screen,
  cut the line and keep the look.
- **Using real dates and logs is powerful and sensitive.** All of it is
  on-device and deterministic; nothing is sent anywhere; the dates are
  only the player's own. Privacy review before shipping Fanus and Emanet.
- **The duo doubles the writing.** About 13 duo exchanges plus island
  scenes; the founder and friends are the bottleneck. Mochi's lines must
  be someone's real aunt, or she becomes a type.
- **Many islands in few weeks.** Fifteen arrivals by day 43 is fast. It is
  what the founder asked for, but it needs Book Two ready at launch + a
  few weeks, and satellites must stay cheap (one plate, a short arc, a
  ritual or a small board set), or the art queue breaks.
- **The metric is a guard, not taste.** Six equality axes are coarse
  (two "dusk" islands may still feel different); passing the tool proves
  no cliff, no repeat and no dead day, not that an island is good.
- **Peggle and the raked loop** both need a pilot before the islands are
  briefed; the raked loop is new and must be proven fun in Rehearsal.
- **Mochi travelling changes home.** Simit at the çay ocağı in the
  afternoon must not read as "Mochi is gone"; she is back every evening.
