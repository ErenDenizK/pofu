# Brief: 1c-5, the pier's levels on Home (2026-11-28)

Role: `.claude/agents/builder.md`. Branch `art/station-pier`. Desk: `[world]`.

## Where it stands

The pier (`StationID.pier`, a harbour ladder in `PofuCore/Game/Archipelago.swift`)
is the last Home station still without levels. The plate paints a full
pier on the harbour islet (the jetty, the ferry and its pool, the lamp
and bell posts, the bunting). `plate-home.json` `stations` has no `pier`,
and `tools/plate/station_looks.py` has no pier spec.

The art is picked (pofu-art #205 and #232; read each job's `review.yaml`
`lead_review:` for the numbers):

- `batch-195-pier-bare-crop`: the bare plot, take t1. It paints out only
  the pier; the gap, the rope bridge, the islet's rim and door, and the
  stations in the window stay.
- `batch-193-plot-ladder-pier`: day levels 1 to 5 are t1, t1, t1, t1, t1;
  nights 1 to 5 are t2 at every level. Each is an edit of the one before,
  in the same 1536 frame, so one cut window serves the ladder.
- Day colour fit: the crop runs hot. Level 1 to the plate is C* x0.72 and
  L* +1.6, and that one fit holds for the ladder (level 5 is about 5 %
  hot, inside tolerance).
- Nights: the il8 night plate's pier window is L* 58.0, C* 18.6. The
  picks sit 4 to 8 L* too dark and a quarter to a third too chromatic.
  The wood has a slight rose-mauve cast.
- **Night 3 needs an edit before the cut.** Both takes light the
  portholes, which level 3 does not have yet (they light at level 4).
  Paint t2's porthole glass and the streaks they cast on the pool the
  unlit blue of the cabin windows.

The template is #550 (1c-3, the bagel cart and the lighthouse on the
harbour islet at stages 1 and 2): plot ladder specs in `station_looks.py`,
erased bases per stage, looks per level, harbour stations counted in
`HomePlate.stationLevels`. #563 added the painted-night path
(`STATION_NIGHT_GRADE`, `PLATE_NIGHT_TARGET`, `station_night_looks`).

## Facts to check first (a false premise is a result)

- Which stages show the pier on the plate, and so which stages need a
  bare base. Measure; do not assume stages 1 and 2.
- The Gezgin moors at Home's pier (`PofuWorld/Gezgin/GezginLayout.swift`,
  `plate-home.json` `mooring`, feet 890, 1100: "the post at the bridge's
  end on the rim"). Does that post survive in the bare crop and at every
  level? If her post goes when the pier is erased, stop and report: an
  unbuilt pier must not leave her tied to nothing.
- `HarborCast.swift` places pieces (horn, lamp, ferry) by the pier on the
  harbour island plate, and the goat's pose follows the pier's level.
  Confirm these are the harbour island's scene, not Home's, so the looks
  do not double them.

## Change and targets

- Pier looks for levels 1 to 5 by day, cut by region from the ladder
  (the bagel cart, lighthouse and tea stall edge in the window are not
  part of the cut), with the one day colour fit.
- Bare bases at the pier's stages, by day and by night (golden at stages
  1 and 2 borrows night, as #560 does).
- Painted nights 1 to 5 graded to the plate's pier (L* within 3 of 58.0,
  C* within 3 of 18.6), the wood without a rose or mauve cast (its hue
  within 5 degrees of the derived look's), night 3's portholes unlit. A
  level whose painted night cannot meet that keeps the derived night.
- At level 0 the pier's plot is the bare crop: no jetty, no ferry.

Stay out of the bagel cart and tea stall entries (`DERIVED_NIGHT`,
`STATION_NIGHT_GRADE` and their picks): a builder on
`art/bagel-night-grade` is changing them now. If it merges first, merge
main. Stay out of `plate-home.json`'s `near` and `seasons` keys.

## Process

- Your own worktree at a new path.
- At most three pushes after the PR opens, with label `screens`.
- Wait for CI with one background command.
- Fetch frames only with `tools/review/fetch_frames.sh`. Disk is about
  8 GB with a 5 GB floor; delete frames as you go. No local `.build`
  beyond what `test-linux.sh` makes.
- Do not merge.

Hand back: the PR number, head SHA and CI result; the crops' paths; the
night numbers per level; the frame-diff list; the budget; what you can
still see.
