## What to build

1. **Spots.** Give Pofu three or four spots on the plate and each
   resident two or three, per island stage where the stage changes what
   stands where. Put them in the plate's data beside `characters`. A
   spot is allowed only if, at every stage and level that can show
   there, no occluder taller than the character covers it: Pofu may
   stand behind the pond, never behind the stall. Check it in a test
   over every stage, not by eye alone.
2. **The move.** A character goes from one spot to the next in two to
   four arcing hops along a straight line inside the walkable polygon,
   then rests. At most one character is moving at a time; a move
   happens at most about twice a minute over all three; never during a
   build moment, a focus, a card, the near lens or bedtime. Reduce
   Motion, Low Power and a still frame mean no moves. Deterministic
   from the day and the visit's seconds, as `PlateHops` is.
3. **What follows a character.** The shadow, the tap target, the card's
   tail, the depth sort against the station looks (`footY`), the speech
   bubble's anchor and the near lens's rest all use where the character
   is now, not the painted spot.
4. **Stop the wasted work.** On the plate, do not run the walk's target
   search; keep the pose, breath and lean. Delete nothing the sprite
   island's own screenshot tests still need.
5. **Lines.** List every character whose card can open in the shipped
   world (Home's residents, Çağlayan's, each visitor). For each one
   without lines, write three with the `pofu-story` skill: Turkish
   first, then English written natively, in the character's voice from
   `content/characters/`. One commit for the lines alone, so the lead
   can read them without the code.
