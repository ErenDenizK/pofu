# Brief 4: a Home that is not silent (2026-11-24)

Stage 3.4 of `docs/design/assessment-2026-11-24-path.md`. The founder's
decision (founder-input 44): ambience on by default, Pofu's voice now,
music later. Small; one PR. Branch: `feat/sound-on`.

Every fact below is a lead. Open it before you build on it.

## What is there

- Ambience is off for anyone who never chose: `SoundSettings.init`
  defaults `isAmbienceOn` to false
  (`Packages/PofuKit/Sources/PofuSound/SoundPolicy.swift:12`) and the
  loader reads `defaults.bool(forKey:)`, which is false for a missing
  key (`:21`). The toggle is in `App/Settings/SettingsView.swift:108-128`.
- Four ambience beds are bundled
  (`PofuSound/Resources/ambience-{hush,wind,rain,crickets}.caf`) and
  conducted by `AmbienceConductor.swift`.
- Pofu has one voice cue in the bundle: `pofuHop`
  (`SoundCue.swift:25`, `pofu-hop.caf`), played from
  `App/WorldSound.swift:115-119`.
- Five voice proofs are not bundled: `design/sound/proof/pofu-chew.wav`,
  `pofu-giggle.wav`, `pofu-poke.wav`, `pofu-sleepy-sigh.wav`,
  `pofu-sniff.wav`; their levels are in `design/sound/proof/levels.json`.
  The sounds are made by `tools/sound/` (`render.py`, `world.py`).
- The rate limits per cue: `SoundPolicy.swift:67`.

## What to build

1. **Ambience on by default.** A player with no stored choice hears
   ambience; a player who turned it off keeps it off. The mute switch
   and the silent switch behave as today.
2. **Pofu's voice.** Bundle the five proofs as cues, converted the way
   the bundled cues were, at the proofs' levels. One moment each:
   - poke: a tap on Pofu;
   - giggle: a second tap soon after, or a cheek poke, in place of the
     poke, never on top of it;
   - chew: when he eats what was collected, beside the gulp, not on it;
   - sleepy sigh: a tap on Pofu at bedtime, in place of the poke;
   - sniff: once when a visitor arrives.
   If a moment does not exist in the code as I describe it, take the
   nearest one that does and name it in the hand-back.
3. Each new cue gets a rate limit so that fast taps never stack voices;
   no two voice cues play at once.
4. Rehearsal mode's sound rows (`TuningLabPanel`) list the new cues, so
   they can be heard one by one.

## Not yours

Music. New ambience beds. The games' sounds. Haptics.

## Acceptance

Sound has no frame, so:

- Tests: a fresh defaults store gives ambience on; a store with the key
  set to false gives off; each new cue resolves to a bundled file; the
  policy never lets two voice cues overlap.
- A table in the PR body: cue, moment, file, level, the line of code
  that plays it.
- Then the founder's phone: a fresh install is not silent, and he hears
  each cue at its moment. His word closes the step.
