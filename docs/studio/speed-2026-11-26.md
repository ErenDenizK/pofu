# Why the studio slowed, and what to change (2026-11-26)

By Fable, for founder-input 46. The numbers are the scout's
(`docs/research/mainflow-2026-11-26/raw-studio-speed.md` on the lead
branch, gathered 11-26 19:11 UTC from the GitHub API); I did not
recompute them. The reading and the proposals are mine. The rule changes
in section 3 are proposals: nothing here edits `CLAUDE.md`,
`docs/studio.md` or an agent file. The plan this serves is
[../design/mainflow-plan-2026-11-26.md](../design/mainflow-plan-2026-11-26.md).

## 1. The short answer

The studio is not slow at building. When work was approved, seven pull
requests merged in 7.5 hours (11-24 19:46 to 11-25 03:14), with a median
of 1.3 minutes between green CI and merge. CI, pushes and small
questions to the founder were problems until mid-November and are
fixed.

The studio is slow at **having approved work**. Four causes, in order of
cost:

1. Nothing runs unless the founder has just written. The lead exists
   only inside his messages, and Codex starts only on his word.
2. The plan runs out. Each plan held a few hours of approved work and
   then waited for him or for an outside date.
3. His verdicts are asked on concept sheets through a relay, and take
   days. The phone, which answers in minutes, has not been used since
   11-14.
4. The art line and the planning went to the wrong things: a mini-game
   board and plans that replaced plans.

## 2. The evidence

### 2.1 Nothing runs between his messages

- **Codex was idle about 82 % of the time.** Gaps of over three hours
  between Codex commits sum to 284 hours of the 347 between its first
  commit (11-12 07:56) and the gather: 65.6, 7.8, 29.1, 9.0, 9.4, 95.1,
  28.6 and 39.8 hours. A long job can run inside a gap, so the true
  figure is somewhat lower; the two longest gaps (95 and 40 hours) have
  no such job.
- **Finished art waited for a merge.** Deliveries #135 to #142 waited
  118 to 121 hours, #164 to #167 and #169 waited 40 to 42 hours, #145 to
  #148 waited 28 to 29. These PRs hold takes that are never promoted, so
  merging them decides nothing.
- **Codex needs a person to start it.** `docs/studio.md` section 9: "The
  founder starts Codex by hand with one word". Every empty queue costs
  one round trip through him.
- **pofu had zero merges for 6.9 days** (11-17 22:45 to 11-24 19:46) and
  none in the 40 hours before the gather. In both, briefs or deliveries
  were waiting and no session was awake.

### 2.2 The plan runs out

- Round 2's path (11-24) was built in 7.5 hours. Its next lines were
  "device days when the membership opens" and "stage 4 held". I wrote
  that hold; it left the studio idle from 11-25 03:14.
- #503 (island progression and the voyage) was written on 11-16, got
  his answers on 11-17, and is still open. #490 (the UI grammar) is open
  since before that. Both are "proposals" that no later plan either
  built or closed.
- Since 11-14 there have been seven plans or assessments (feel and
  world, world presentation, the night plan, island progression, board
  and upgrade, the concept round, the two-round assessment). This page's
  plan is the eighth. Each restarted the order instead of amending it.

### 2.3 Verdicts on sheets, late

- The four topic sheets went to him on 11-18 11:58. His verdict reached
  the lead on 11-23: five days, and it "never reached the lead before"
  (fi 43).
- The rule since fi 40 was "no code on a topic until he picks from the
  frames". The result: 205 concept jobs in three rounds; of seven
  topics, the board and the station card reached the app. His verdict on
  round 1 was that it was too flashy, which a pilot of five jobs would
  have shown.
- `docs/now.md`: "Founder last played: 11-14". The free build installs
  from `main` without the membership. In twelve days of work on how
  things look and feel, nothing was judged where it is seen.
- `docs/studio.md` section 7 already says "one question, a picture, a
  default: if no answer, I go on with A". The concept-first rule
  overrode it: there was no default, so no answer meant no work.

### 2.4 Effort on the wrong things

- **The board.** By PR title 12 of 99 pofu PRs since 11-12 are the
  mini-game; in the art line it is 19 of 49 merged deliveries since
  11-15 and 124 of 514 job folders (24 %). Eight batches and 71 jobs
  came before the board that shipped, which was then rebuilt the next
  day (#514, #518).
- **Stale work in sight.** 59 open PRs in pofu-art (the oldest from
  11-12) and nine in pofu, none touched since 11-17. Every resume reads
  past them.

### 2.5 What is not the problem

- CI: about 35 to 40 minutes a full run; pushes per PR fell from a mean
  of 4.2 to 1.6.
- Small questions to him: four in 11-14 and 11-15, none found since.
- The lead's bookkeeping: `now.md` took 50 commits on 11-16 and takes 2
  to 7 a day now.
- Agent count: the limit that binds is one Swift build at a time, and it
  was not reached on any idle day.

## 3. The changes

Six, in order of effect. Each has the rule text to add or replace; the
lead applies what the founder approves.

### Change 1: the lead wakes by itself

A scheduled task wakes the lead's session every three hours, day and
night, and on activity in pofu-art. On each wake it does four things and
stops: merge finished deliveries; refill the Codex queue if it holds
less than a day of work; start the next brief of the plan if a lane is
free; write one line in `now.md`.

```diff
 ## 6. Memory and your own context
+- **The studio runs without a message.** A scheduled task wakes the
+  lead every three hours. On a wake: merge deliveries, check the queue
+  depth, start the next brief if a lane is free, note it in `now.md`.
+  A wake that finds nothing to do says so in one line and ends.
```

*Cost:* a few short lead turns a day. *Founder's part:* approving that
the session may wake itself.

### Change 2: Codex never needs "çalış" twice

Deliveries of takes merge on green checks without a look (a look is
needed to pick, not to merge). The queue is kept at two days of work,
with the plan's next step always queued before the current step ends.
Codex's own loop reads `QUEUE.md` again from `origin/main` when it
finishes a batch and goes on. The last part is a change to `AGENTS.md`
in pofu-art, which I did not read in full: the lead checks whether the
loop already does this and what stops it.

```diff
 ## 9. The art factory
-- **Two tiers.** Demand: what the app is waiting for; keep at least two
-  READY rows; review each delivery within a day.
+- **Two tiers.** Demand: what the app is waiting for; keep at least two
+  days of READY rows (count jobs, not rows). A delivery of takes merges
+  on green checks at the next wake; its picks are reviewed when the
+  step that uses it starts.
+- **Idle is an incident.** Twelve hours without a Codex commit while
+  READY rows exist gets a row in `friction.md` with its cause.
```

### Change 3: verdicts on the phone, with a default and a time

Replace "concept first, he picks, then code" with: one pilot of two to
five jobs, the lead picks, it is built, and he sees it in the app. A
question to him carries a picture, the lead's pick and a time; when the
time passes, the pick stands.

```diff
 ## 7. The founder
-- **One question at a time, with a picture and a default:** "Cevap
-  gelmezse A ile ilerliyorum." Ask only about major direction, with
-  options that truly differ.
+- **One question at a time, with a picture, a default and a time:**
+  "Yarın 12:00'ye kadar cevap gelmezse A ile ilerliyorum." Ask only
+  about major direction, with options that truly differ. No work waits
+  for an answer: the default is built meanwhile, and a different answer
+  is a change, not a restart.
+- **He judges in the app.** A look is shown to him as a build on `main`
+  and three things to look at, not as a concept sheet. Concept rounds
+  are not run; a new look gets one pilot of two to five jobs, picked by
+  the lead.
+- **After each step's note he plays for five minutes.** `now.md`
+  records the day he last played; three days without it is the first
+  line of the next message to him.
```

*Founder's part:* this reverses his "konsept önce, sonra native" of
fi 40. It is his to accept.

### Change 4: one plan, amended

The main-flow plan is the route until its six steps are done. A new
wish from him becomes a row in it (a step, or a line in a step), not a
new plan. No step is "held" behind an outside date; a step that cannot
start names the step that runs instead. Fable is called for a step's
spec when the step before merges, not for a new assessment.

```diff
 ## 3. A chapter
+- **The plan never ends in a wait.** A plan's last line is work that
+  can start. "Held" and "waits for the founder" are allowed only beside
+  a named piece of work that runs meanwhile.
+- **A plan is amended, not replaced.** While
+  `docs/design/mainflow-plan-2026-11-26.md` has open steps, new
+  direction is a dated row in it.
```

### Change 5: one channel to the lead

His verdict on the sheets was lost for five days in the relay. Whatever
the side channel is, its output must land in the repository where a
wake reads it: the side channel commits his message to
`docs/product/founder-input.md` on the lead branch, or he writes to the
lead session directly. A wake reads that file's tail first.

*Founder's part:* which of the two he prefers.

### Change 6: clear the stale work once

Close #503 and #490 into the plan (their content is in steps 2 and 4),
close #507, #461 and #453 as superseded, and close or merge the 59 open
pofu-art PRs in one pass: a delivery of takes merges, anything else
closes with one line. One builder, half a day, no judgement beyond
"is it takes".

## 4. What I would not change

- The cap of three agents and one Swift build.
- The push limit and the rule to look at 1:1 crops.
- The lead deciding process calls alone (fi 31, 37): it works.
- Codex painting no interface.

## 5. What this should give, and how to tell

If changes 1 and 2 hold, Codex's idle share falls from about 82 % to
under 25 %, and no delivery waits more than three hours. If 3 and 4
hold, pofu merges something on every day he is not blocking, and "last
played" is never more than three days old. The scout's script-free
method (section headers of the raw file) can be rerun in a week; the
four numbers to compare are: Codex gaps over three hours, delivery
created-to-merged p90, days with zero pofu merges, and the days since he
played.
