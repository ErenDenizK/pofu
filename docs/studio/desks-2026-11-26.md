## 0. What he asked, and what is in the way

"Burası bir oyun geliştirme stüdyosu." Many lines of work, each deep,
each with its own method: music, game, art, the rest of the app. He
wants them to run at once, each with its own plan, context, route and
record, with no line losing its thread, nothing left out, and the whole
moving together, inside the limits of cost, context and his time.

What is in the way is one shape, seen four times:

1. **One context carries every line.** The lead's rules are at their
   caps: `docs/studio.md` is 4,495 words of 4,500 and `CLAUDE.md` 81
   lines of 80 [seen]. A new line of work (music) has nowhere to put
   its method. Sound, motion, the calendar, the gate and release have
   no desk row at all [scout].
2. **One session is the clock.** When the lead sleeps nothing merges
   and nothing starts: 40 hours without a merge before #519, Codex idle
   about 40 hours, deliveries waiting on a merge that decides nothing
   (the speed page, 2.1). The speed page's six changes merged as
   proposals in #519, and none is in `docs/studio.md` yet [seen: no
   line of them in the lead branch's copy]: the fix for waiting waited.
3. **The machines that need no session are switched off.** GitHub
   auto-merge is off in both repositories, `main` has no required
   check, and `pofu-art` has no workflow [seen: API]. A test-accepted
   pull request therefore needs someone awake to press merge.
4. **One lane is treated as the only lane.** "One Swift build at a
   time" is true, and it has quietly become "one thing at a time".
   Music renders, art ingests, story lines and docs need no Swift
   build.

So the answer is not more agents or a second lead. It is to take the
desks out of the lead's head, the clock out of the lead's session, and
the lanes out of each other's way.

## 1. What "done" means

1. He opens one page in Turkish and sees every line of work in a line
   each: what moved, what is next, what waits for him.
2. He writes `Masa: müzik` in a new session, and that session knows the
   music line's plan, method and history without reading anything else,
   and without the lead relaying.
3. He sleeps eight hours and finds, in the morning: Codex's deliveries
   merged, test-accepted work merged, the next row of each free lane
   started, and at most three cards waiting for him.
4. Adding a line (marketing, say) is writing one book, and costs the
   lead's context nothing.

## 3. What it costs

- **Context.** A desk agent loads `CLAUDE.md` (81 lines) and its book
  (at most 140). Today a builder's brief re-states what a charter would
  hold, 40 lines at a time, and the lead writes it each time.
- **The lead.** `docs/studio.md` falls from 4,495 to 4,429 words with
  the patch (the art factory's rules move to the art desk's charter),
  and the lead stops carrying desk detail in `now.md`.
- **Money.** No standing cost: no new session runs unless he opens one.
  A wake is one short lead turn. Auto-merge and the workflow cost no
  tokens at all.
- **Fable.** Writes a desk's charter and first plan once (the first of
  its kind); Opus and Sonnet run it after.

With (a), (b) and (d) alone, art and tested code move while every
session sleeps. What still needs a waking lead is work an eye must
accept, and starting the next row. That is what (c) is for.

`pofu-art/.github/workflows/merge-takes.yml`, proposed (untested: no
workflow has run in that repository):

```yaml
name: merge-takes
on:
  pull_request:
    types: [opened, synchronize, reopened]
    branches: [main]
permissions:
  contents: write
  pull-requests: write
jobs:
  merge:
    if: startsWith(github.head_ref, 'art/')
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - name: Merge a delivery that holds only takes
        env:
          GH_TOKEN: ${{ github.token }}
          PR: ${{ github.event.pull_request.number }}
          REPO: ${{ github.repository }}
        run: |
          files=$(gh api "repos/$REPO/pulls/$PR/files" --paginate --jq '.[].filename')
          if echo "$files" | grep -qv '^design/art/takes/'; then
            echo "Not only takes: left for the lead."; exit 0
          fi
          gh pr merge "$PR" --repo "$REPO" --merge
```
