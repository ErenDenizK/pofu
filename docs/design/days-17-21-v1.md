## 1. What "done" means

On the Prova path (one "Boş ver" a day, "Next day") the founder meets one
new thing on each of days 17 to 21, and each is something he hears or sees,
not only reads:

| Day | He opens Pofu and | What he feels |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | after "Topla", Pofu says his humming left the island for the first time; the button is "Mırıldan" and four notes sound | Pofu has a song, and I asked for it |
| 18 | "Dinle": the four notes, a pause, one lower note from far off | something out there answered |
| 19 | "Dinle": two notes come back; Mochi's tea shivered | it is learning the song |
| 20 | "Dinle": all four come back, the third one wrong; Pofu shouted the right one | whoever it is cannot hear well, and is close to having it |
| 21 | the bell's card (unchanged text) and the far bell plays the song right, then one note more; "Ben de duydum" | I did hear it: the answer is true now |

Each night of days 17 to 20 Pofu's bedtime line listens instead of
repeating, and day 21's says "İyi geceler". With PR 2 a small warm light
stands among the painted stars on those nights, a little stronger each
night, and each far note is a soft ring of light in the sky, so the notes
are seen with the ringer off.

No streak, no day count on screen, nothing that expires, no seeds spent:
a day not played keeps its step for the next day played, and the bell
still waits until heard (`FarBell.swift:9-10`).

Notes. The scene: a private habit gets an answer; nobody says "someone is
listening". The object is the four notes; the change is how many come
back. Devices are Pofu's own: the body and the house (snoring, tea, an
ear), one parenthetical. Checked against the tics list: no theme word
("sessiz", "huzur"), no "sanki", no day count, no exclamation mark.
Unsure: line 4's three short sentences in a row
(deadpan, but terse), and whether "Sen uyu" reads as care or as advice.
The last line ends the trial on "İyi geceler", as the book does (story map
§1.3), with no hook.

**`FarBellPrelude.swift`** (pure, `PofuCore`):

- `public enum FarBellPrelude` with `static let steps = 4`;
  `static func step(tendedDays: Int, bothHealed: Bool, bell: FarBellStatus) -> Int?`:
  `nil` unless `bothHealed` and `bell == .silent`; `left = FarBell.tendedDays − tendedDays`;
  `nil` unless `1...steps` contains `left`; else `steps − left + 1`.
  `static func step(in world: WorldSnapshot) -> Int?` passes
  `world.koke.isHealed && world.caglayan.isHealed`.
  `static func isBellDay(in world: WorldSnapshot) -> Bool`:
  `world.bell != .silent && world.tendedDays == FarBell.tendedDays`.
- `public enum FarBellTune` with
  `struct Note: Hashable, Sendable { var step: Int; var at: TimeInterval; var isFar: Bool }`
  (`step` is the pentatonic ladder step of `SoundCue.bloom(step:)`: 0 is D4, 5 is D5) and
  constants `hum = [5, 7, 8, 9]`, `farSong = [0, 2, 3, 4]`, `wrongThird = 1`,
  `oneMore = 5`, `answered = [0, 1, 2, 4]`, `humGap = 0.34`, `farGap = 0.46`,
  `breath = 0.9`, `bellLead = 1.4`.
  `static func prelude(step: Int) -> [Note]`: empty outside 1...4; the hum at
  `0, humGap, 2·humGap, 3·humGap` (not far); then the first `answered[step − 1]`
  notes of `farSong` (far), the first at `3·humGap + breath`, each `farGap` later;
  at step 4 the third far note's step is `wrongThird`.
  `static func bell() -> [Note]`: `farSong + [oneMore]`, all far, at
  `bellLead + i·farGap`. `static func length(of: [Note]) -> TimeInterval`:
  the last `at`, 0 when empty.
