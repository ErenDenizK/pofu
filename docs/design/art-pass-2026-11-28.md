## 1. What "done" means (the bar)

As the founder sees it on his iPhone 17:

1. **Night is a cool, deep, coloured night, not grey.** The lawn is a
   dark cool green with colour in it, the flowers sit in the moonlight
   with the lawn, the windows and lanterns glow, and Pofu and the
   residents are lit by the same night (Home and near).
2. **The golden-hour sky is pink, not violet,** with no flat cut-out
   cloud shapes and no mirrored edges; the island under it is warm.
3. **Pofu stands on the ground** at Home and up close: a soft shadow at
   his feet, his peach as strong as the character card, never perched on
   a lantern post, and **never more than two hands**.
4. **The near camera is sharp where it looks:** a soft foreground that
   reads as painted shapes, at most a quarter of the screen; far islands
   hazed, not smeared into rainbow blobs.
5. **Pofu's states are paintings,** one per state, cut from one approved
   master on the corrected palette; props are drawn once and held in
   every pose of a family.
6. **Winter is visibly winter** once its art lands; until then a gate
   says so in every report instead of letting it pass silently.
7. Seeds never buy screen time and Pofu never dies (ADR-0015, ADR-0017):
   nothing here touches the economy, the gate or care; no state, pose or
   light makes Pofu look ill, grey or gone.

## 2. Where we stand (evidence)

**The Home picture is the painting; nothing grades it.** On Home the
plate, its sky set and its residents are drawn outside the world grade:
`IslandScene.swift:806-808` ("The painted plate, never graded"),
`PlateHomeViews.swift:11`; the residents take `.neutral` on the plate
(`IslandScene.swift:843`), and so does Pofu (`IslandScene.swift:2157`).
They take the plate's own light instead (`HomePlate.swift:366-398`,
`harmony` and `lightMap` in `plate-home.json`). The code sky
(`LivingSky`, fed by `colour-script.json`) draws only when no painted
plate or sky is fully in (`IslandScene.swift:747-748`). So the colour
script's cells reach Home only through the tools that bake its files,
never at runtime. This is ADR-0023 (a): "The runtime applies no grade,
LUT, fog or vignette to a plate in any state."

**Night.** Home's night is ten painted plates (`plate-home.json`
`painted.night` = stages 0 to 9). Lawn boxes on `path-d21-0900` against
`path-d21-2300` (same stage and camera; boxes 40 x 30 at (260,1130),
(1040,1190), (600,1250), (1060,1230)): day L82-85 C52-56 h95-121, night
L62-70 C10-20 h95-125. The night keeps its value (ADR-0021 §2 asks for
that) but loses about 75 % of its chroma. The picked level-8 night take
measures the same in the file (`design/art/takes/batch-72-home-night/x-home-flow-il8-night/plate__x-home-flow-il8-night--t2.webp`,
box 400,880,640,930: L58 C9 h166), while the founder-liked night
(`batch-22-gezgin-forms/x-gezgin-f2-night/plate__x-gezgin-f2-night--t1.webp`,
box 450,170,800,230) is L53 C29 h100. The grey is in the paint.
Everything downstream follows it by design: the near foreground strip's
night tint is measured from the night plate's lawn
(`tools/plate/near_ground_data.py:41-57`; `plate-home.json`
`near.foreground.tint.night` = 0.472, 0.583, 0.925), the characters'
night light caps their chroma to the lawn's ("night's lawn is nearly
grey", `tools/plate/harmony_check.py:26`, `:31`), and the station night
looks are graded to the level-8 night plate's station means
(`tools/plate/station_looks.py:1352` `PLATE_NIGHT_TARGET`; derived nights
fit day-to-night on the plate pair, `:1356`, `:1361`). Near at night:

C10. The lantern pool under a character is `pool` 0.35 in every night
harmony entry.

**Seasons.** `plate-home.json` `seasons` = `autumn` only, `complete:
false`, states day 0 and night 0. Winter is not listed, so a winter day
