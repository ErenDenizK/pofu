# Island healing arc

**Status:** rule by the lead, 2026-11-05 (founder's note). Applies to every
wonder island's concepts, stage art and code, and to home's fog and
harbor. Codex checks it before drawing any "stuck" or "healed" island.

## The founder's note

The concepts so far go from "medium" to "very good". He wants "bad to
good": not ruined, and not "a few lights came on" either. The feeling of
healing must grow in both directions (a clearer wound at the start, a
bigger reward at the end), with no exaggeration, planned island by
island.

## The rule

| Stage | Reads as | Colour and light | Life |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Stuck | something is clearly wrong, and you can name it in one word, and it is still beautiful: arriving keeps its magic (founder, 2026-11-06) | **value over chroma** ([sky and weather v1](sky-and-weather-v1.md) §2.4): warm hues drained to ~50 %, but a full value range, rich cool shade and real fog; ONE warm focal light, no practical lit | one sign of life only (a sprout, a blink), caught by that light, so it is sad, not dead |
| 1-2 | the wound is being mended, one named element per stage | chroma +15 % per stage; the first warm light appears at stage 2 | a resident peeks out |
| 3 | almost there: the shape is whole, the soul is missing | near full colour, still no signature | the resident is active |
| 4 Healed | better than the player imagined | full colour, warm practicals, the island's one signature image moving | everyone, plus a gift for home |

- **One wound, one cure.** Each island's damage is one specific thing
  (water stopped, bells silent, moss asleep), not general decay. Each
  stage fixes a visible part of that one thing.
- **Gentle first, braver later.** Early islands are dim, still, quiet, a
  little overgrown or dry. From the middle of the book an island may be
  eerie (see the variety table): a ruin, an unsettling sound, a fog that
  hides something. Never gore, never a jump scare, never a dead creature;
  the eerie part is a mood, and it always heals.
- **The signature is held back** until stage 4: the waterfall pours, the
  aurora moves, the bells ring. Nothing before it hints at full strength.
- **Measured, not guessed:** harmony's chroma between stage 0 and 4
  should differ by at least 2x; a lit-pixel ratio confirms the light
  steps. Codex records both in the review.

## Variety: no two islands sick the same way

The founder (2026-11-05, a view, not an order): do not apply one decision
to every island; spread the moods, let a few be a little scary or ruined,
more as the game goes on, even if hidden; experiment and drop what fails.

| Island | Register of the wound | Senses | Experiment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Koke | sleepy, sad | quiet snoring | none: the gentle start |
| Çağlayan | dry, abandoned | silence where water should be | a hollow echo when you tap the dry rocks |
| Emanet | **eerie fog** | muffled sounds, a shape that moves in the fog (only lost things) | the fog parts where you look; first island with a hint of unease |
| Çanlar (filler) | odd, funny | **a wrong note**: one bell rings off-key at night | a playful creep, solved with a joke |
| Revontuli | **ruin** | wind through broken lantern towers | half-fallen towers rebuilt, the most visible repair in the book |
| Arı | tired | a low hum, no bees | none: a breather before the end |
| Mehtap | **dread, quiet** | stars going out one by one, a cold sound | the darkest moment of the book, right before the finale |

Concepts try the braver version first and the lead pulls it back if it
scares more than it charms; the device test decides.

## Per island (Book One)

| Island | Wound (stage 0) | Stages 1-3 mend | Healed signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Koke | a grey, sleeping moss hump; the island's flowers closed | moss turns green patch by patch, flowers open, Koke's eye opens | Koke stands up and the island blooms around it |
| Çağlayan | the falls stopped: dry rock steps, a still pool, a mossy wheel | one stream reconnects per stage, the pool fills, the wheel turns | the waterfall pours, with a rainbow mist |
| Emanet | lost things piled in fog, unlabeled, a closed shed | piles become shelves, labels return, the shed opens | lantern-lit shelves, every thing in its place, residents collect |
| Çanlar (filler) | bells hang mute and tangled | ropes untangled one by one | the four-note chord rings (sound first) |
| Revontuli | a black night sky, an unlit lantern post | lanterns lit along a path | the aurora moves over the island |
| Arı | an empty hive, closed flowers | bees return in groups, flowers open in colour bands | the swarm's dance over Ayçiçeği |
| Mehtap | stars missing from a dull sky | each stage joins a constellation | the moon trail reaches home |
| Gezgin (Nazlı) | form 1: tired, faded, a lantern hook empty | a detail on her back per main island | form 2: grown, lush, the hook and scar glow |

Home already follows this (the harbor's fog lifts on day 3; the island
level ladder). Next concept rounds (batch-34 variations and every island
