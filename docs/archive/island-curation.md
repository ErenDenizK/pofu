# Island Curation: Arcs, Order and a Merciless Review

_Status: proposal for discussion with the founder. Date: 2026-11-03.
Builds on [archipelago-system.md](archipelago-system.md) v2 (section 1A:
home cluster, uniqueness contract, living web, pacing), the founder's
ratings of 2026-11-03 in [founder-input.md](../product/founder-input.md) (Gezgin 10,
Revontuli 7, Çanlar and Emanet "great", Koke 6, Çömlek 4, Yıldız 3,
Leylek 2, Ayna "redo") and [minigames.md](../product/minigames.md). Same day he
added that the concept paintings are very good overall, Gezgin and
Revontuli most of all. Nothing here changes code; where this document
disagrees with archipelago-system.md section 11, this is the proposal and
section 11 changes only after the founder agrees._

His ask, translated: evaluate the island concepts, their order and the
logic of progression systematically and with examples; review every
concept mercilessly; no cliffs between islands, similar where it should
be, different where it should be, always exciting; every island should
**develop**, like One Piece: improvements, problems solved, stories that
end.

## 0. The answer

1. **The v2 islands are places with secrets; they need to be stories
   that end.** Today most five-stage lists are build lists ("the stones;
   the clacker; a tea house; a maple branch; the answer"), and six of ten
   end on the **same** reveal (the thing here exists to bring lost
   islands home). Every island now gets **a wound, a resident's want, a
   turn and a bloom**: arrive at something stopped, silent, cold or
   sealed; leave when it pours, rings, glows or opens. The signature
   motion is the island's progress bar: faint at arrival, full at stage 5.
2. **The myth is told once per island, a sentence at a time, never
   answered ten times.** Gezgin owns the big reveal; the others each add
   one clue (section 5).
3. **Verdicts:** ship Gezgin, Çanlar, Revontuli, Koke, Emanet, Mehtap;
   rework Çağlayan (the opener must be an 8, and today it is a 5), Çömlek
   (kept for its mole and wheel) and Fanus (it duplicates Koke and
   Çağlayan inside its glass); **cut Leylek as an island** (it is Gezgin's
   weaker twin, its stork-carries-a-bundle image is the baby-delivery
   cliché, and its game breaks our own no-timing rule) and keep the storks
   as a home event; promote **Arı** (the hanging hive) from the idea pool;
   park Kaplıca (Çağlayan and Revontuli's sauna already own warm water)
   and Kitap (year two).
4. **Order:** Çağlayan, Gezgin, then a choice of **Revontuli or Koke**,
   Çanlar as the first finale, Emanet, then a choice of **Fanus or Arı**,
   Çömlek, and Mehtap as the year's finale on a real full moon. Each step
   keeps three things and changes two or three; no step changes nothing
   and none changes everything (section 4).

### 1.1 The rules for Pofu

1. **Arrive at a wound, leave at a bloom.** Every island is a story that
   ends (One Piece, BotW's regions). The wound is gentle (something
   stopped, silent, cold, lost, sealed, unable), never a villain, sickness
   or death. The **signature motion is the progress bar**: a trickle on
   day one, the full fall at stage 5.
2. **Keep three, change two or three.** Pofu, the loop, the camera and
   one thread from the last island stay; two or three uniqueness axes
   change (Odyssey's unchanged moves plus one capture). Zero changed is a
   repeat; everything changed is a cliff.
3. **One verb, taught in four beats.** Introduce, develop, twist,
   conclude across the stages (kishōtenketsu). Never two verbs on one
   island.
4. **Alternate the texture.** Brainy and juicy games, warm and cool
   palettes, day and night skies, small and huge islands take turns, the
   way Odyssey alternates big and small kingdoms. Two neighbours never
   share a palette family, and never three in a row share a game texture.
5. **Old allies return.** Every island leaves a thread that a later
   island pulls (One Piece callbacks, Pikmin's re-opened areas). The web
   is the plot, not only the economy.
6. **Every arc ends with a feast at home.** At stage 5 the resident takes
   the vapur to the çay ocağı for a tea party, and leaves a permanent
   gift (ACNH's ceremonies, Kirby's town, One Piece's banquets). Home is
   the scrapbook.
7. **Always one far landmark.** One mystery visible all year that no
   single island answers (Hawk Peak, the castle): the outer ring in the
   fog, and Pofu's own lullaby (section 5).
8. **Space the peaks; finales are convergence islands.** Every third or
   fourth island pays off the ones before it (Çanlar rings all their
   motifs; Çömlek fires all their goods; Mehtap maps them). Peaks never
   sit side by side, and a valley is never two islands long.

## 2. The arc template

Every wonder island is briefed with this table in addition to the
schema of archipelago §3. A writer fills the right column; a reviewer
rejects any row left vague.

| Beat | What it is | Rules | Example (Çağlayan, reworked) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rumours** (before) | Sound, object, sight (archipelago §9.3) | The object is a clue to the **wound**, not only to the place | A bottle on the pier, half full of dry travertine sand |
| **Arrival hook** | The 15–20 s reveal (§3.2) | The signature motion starts on camera **weakly**: the promise, not the payoff | The island arrives; a thin trickle runs over the lowest rim and stops. Damla holds an empty cup |
| **The wound** | What is wrong, in one picture | Gentle; visible in the plate; fixable by attention, never by force | The fall has stopped; the pools are dry white stone |
| **The resident's want** | A small, slightly silly desire | Comic, specific, the character's personality | Damla wants to catch the waterfall in her cup "just once" |
| **The resident's need** | The tender thing under the want | Never spoken directly before stage 3 | She thinks the fall stopped because she tried to catch it |
| **What the player does** | The island game (rounds earn material), the toy, decor spots, requests | One verb; the toy earns nothing; requests point backward only | Route leaf-boat channels; each solved pool fills with water again |
| **Stage 1: Meet** | Wound shown, game introduced | Introduce the verb in its simplest form | Clear the first channel; one pool fills; a shy trickle returns |
| **Stage 2: First help** | A small visible fix | Develop: a new piece | The spring behind the rim is found; a second pool; curved stones appear |
| **Stage 3: Turn** | A discovery reframes the wound | Twist: a new rule; the need is spoken | A leaf boat from home's pond is wedged in the spring: the water was blocked, not offended. Damla laughs at herself. Locks appear in the game |
| **Stage 4: Gathering** | Other islands help | A new place; the web's goods arrive (only from earlier islands, so stage 4 here asks home) | Simit sends planks for a mill race; the mill turns "for no reason, it just likes turning" |
| **Stage 5: Bloom** | The island heals visibly | The signature motion at full; the game's final twist; the resident changes | The full fall pours off the rim into the clouds, longer than the island is tall, with a mist rainbow. Damla finally catches some in her cup, and gives it away |
| **Feast** | The resident visits home for tea | Mochi serves in that culture's way; one line from Pofu | Damla at the çay ocağı, cup on the table |
| **Parting gift** | Keepsake for the den, permanent gift to home (1A.4) | Fixed, named, never random | Keepsake: the cup that never empties. Home: morning rainbows after calm nights |
| **Thread** | One unanswered thing pointing to a later island | Paid off two to six islands later | The rainbow points north at dusk (Revontuli); "the water comes back as rain somewhere far" (Koke) |
| **Living after** | Seasonal return, awake days, a late secret | See below | Autumn: leaves float in the boats; a late secret after Mehtap: the fall freezes for one full-moon night |

**How a finished island keeps living.** Odyssey's Moon Rocks and One
Piece's returning allies, in Pofu's rules (nothing expires, A7):

- **A seasonal beat a year**: one plate edit or grade per season the
  island cares about (Koke's maple, Revontuli's midsummer night sun,
  Çağlayan's autumn leaves on the boats). It returns every year.
- **Awake days** (1A.4): a request, a visitor at home, a weather moment.
- **Late secrets**: when a later island arrives, it may unlock one
  **epilogue beat** on an older one (a sixth, small stage: a new game
  piece and a scene). Gezgin's whale moors at Çağlayan's falls after
  Mehtap; Zil hangs a bell on Koke's tree after Çanlar. This is the
  "callback" that makes old islands feel alive and makes the world feel
  connected.
- **Cameos**: residents appear on other islands' plates in later stages
  (Damla fishing at Revontuli's hole). Cheap (a sprite on a socket),
  enormously warm.

### 3.1 Gezgin, the island on a whale. Ship.

**Why it works.** The only island that is alive; the best silhouette we
have; the painting already earns a 10. Slide (Rush Hour, Unblock Me) is
a proven, quiet, one-thumb puzzle whose fiction ("clear the cloud banks
so she can swim on") is exactly what the verb does.

**What is weak.** The story as written ends on "she carries lost islands
home", the same sentence Çanlar, Emanet, Fanus and Çömlek also end on.
It belongs to Gezgin alone. The moving whale is a checking risk (already
flagged in open question 12). As the first Plus island under cut C2, the
best island sits behind the wall; that is deliberate, and the reason the
free island before it must be strong.

**Arc.** Wound: Nazlı is tired and has lost the way; Yosun's satchel is
full of letters nobody could deliver. Want (Yosun): to deliver one
letter on time, once in his life. Turn (stage 3): the letters are from
islands still lost in the far waters; Nazlı was carrying them home and
fell asleep. Bloom: she sings, and her song is **the same four notes as
Pofu's lullaby** (thread, section 5); she begins circling the
archipelago. Feast: Yosun arrives at the çay ocağı a day late and very
proud. Thread: the undelivered letters (Emanet), the song (Çanlar, the
far landmark).

**Fixes.** Own the myth reveal here and nowhere else. Keep the whale's
face as in the painting (closed eyes, soft smile); never open the mouth.
Night-only movement as planned.

### 3.2 Revontuli, the fox-fire island. Ship, with a new game.

**Why it works.** A new colour world (night, snow, aurora) after two
daylit islands, a real culture (*revontulet*, fox fires), a resident who
is serious about saunas. The founder loves this painting.

**What is weak, mercilessly.** Its games are not games. Painting the
aurora with a stroke has no decision and no challenge: it is a **toy**.
Ice fishing ("put the phone down for 20 minutes") is a beautiful
**ritual** that fits the product, but it is not a round. So the island
has two half-games and no game. And the painting he loves is the banned
template with snow on it (lawn oval, soil bowl, roots): the contract's
ice floe must keep everything he liked (spruce, hut, fox, round hole,
aurora) or we lose the charm while gaining the silhouette.

**Fix: Peggle under the ice.** The round game becomes the proven aim and
drop of Peggle (and of its ancestor, pachinko): aim a lantern float and
let it fall through the frozen lake; every ice bubble it touches lights
up in aurora green, violet or rose; ten floats a round; when the round
ends, **the bubbles you lit become tonight's aurora** over the island
(and, after stage 5, over home on calm winter nights). One thumb, no
timing, juicy, a natural end, and the aurora gets a real verb behind it.
The stroke-painting becomes the **toy** (sweep Tuli's tail; a streak of
light follows and fades). Ice fishing stays as the island's **ritual**
(phone down, something has bitten). Guard against pachinko's casino
roots: no multiplier slots, no fever jackpot, no random bonus buckets;
the yield is deterministic from bubbles lit (A17).

**Arc.** Wound: the aurora has not come for a long time. Want (Tuli):
the best sauna in the north. Need: the legend says a fox paints the sky;
Tuli is a young fox and has never managed it, and believes she cannot.
Turn: the fox tracks up the snow drift are her own, from every night she
tried. Bloom: on the player's next calm night (A21 keeps it to an
arrival after 06:00, shown as "last night"), the sky is painted, and it
was her. Feast: Tuli at the çay ocağı asks for her tea in a sauna. Thread:
the aurora reaches home; a single green streak points to the far ring.

**Pilot.** Two plates side by side: the floe, and the loved painting
with only the underside replaced by glowing ice. The founder picks.

### 3.3 Çanlar, the island of bells. Ship as the first finale.

**Why it works.** A spire is a perfect silhouette and the painting is
already strong. Placed fifth, it is a **convergence island**: each bell
plays the motif of an island the player has, so the archipelago turns
into one song (One Piece's Skypiea bell, rung to say "we are here").

**What is weak.** Simon is proven but thin: three rounds in, it is a
memory test. Most phones play muted, so a sound-only Simon fails half
the players. And "a slightly deaf, very proud" bell-ringer in a sound
game risks reading as a joke at deafness.

**Fixes.** Zil **feels the bells through her paws**: she watches them
swing and feels them in the stone, so the game is fully visual (each
bell swings, glows its colour, shakes its bunting) and sound is a bonus.
Her trait becomes dignified and the reason for the visual design.
Deepen Simon over the stages (kishōtenketsu): stage 2 longer phrases;
stage 3 call and response (Zil rings a question, you choose the answer
phrase); stage 4 two bells at once (harmony); stage 5 the full song of
every island you have. Wound: the bells are silent and tangled. Bloom:
all of them ring, and every awake island answers with a puff of colour
on the map.

### 3.4 Emanet, the lost-and-found. Ship.

**Why it works.** The heart of the archipelago: the island is its
memory. Boncuk the magpie who must give back everything shiny is a
perfect want/need split in one character.

**What is weak.** Its daily deduction is a **content treadmill**: a
Wordle-shaped puzzle a day means 365 authored puzzles a year, in two
languages. The silhouette "built from lost things" (umbrella roof, teacup
tank, top hat chimney) is clever but at high risk of reading as clutter
at 60 pt.

**Fixes.** Generate the puzzle, author the clues' grammar: a small logic
grid ("Murdle" lite) built from the keepsake and goods catalogue (owner,
island, colour, size), with hand-written clue templates in both
languages. Keep it once a day, as planned. For the silhouette, one
dominant object must win: the **upturned umbrella** as the whole roof,
read first; everything else is small. Arc: wound: Boncuk keeps
everything and cannot let go (the shelves are full). Turn (stage 3):
she keeps them because nobody ever came back for them. Bloom: the back
room's map of the outer ring, and the last unclaimed object on the top
shelf is **Pofu's** (section 5). Feast: Boncuk returns Mochi's spoon in
person. Art pilot first (two silhouettes at 60 pt).

### 3.5 Koke, the bonsai in the rain. Ship.

**Why it works.** The redesign is right: a tree larger than its land is
a new silhouette, and a rainy sky is a real "its own sky". Triple-tile
matching (Tile Busters, Triple Match) is one of the most proven casual
mechanics; without the slot-bar fail state it is calm and still juicy.

**What is weak.** The story is a build list; "the patterns are letters"
is a good idea hidden at stage 5. The concept painting is a Japanese
garden **on the template** (lawn oval, soil bowl, round door): a 6 for a
reason. Cultural risk: torii, cherry blossom and Mount Fuji would make
it a postcard.

**Arc.** Wound: the gravel moves at night, and nobody answers. Want
(Sumi): to stay awake through one whole winter. Need: she rakes letters
to the other islands every night and has never had a reply. Turn
(stage 3): the patterns are letters; they have been read, the islands
were too far to answer. Bloom: the first reply arrives, as goods on the
vapur (Çağlayan's water for the tea house); the tree drops one red maple
leaf on the pond. Feast: Sumi at the çay ocağı, apologising for
yawning. Thread: Revontuli's ice for the tea house (if Revontuli came
first) or a promise "the cold one will write" (if not). **No torii, no
sakura, no Fuji**; the learning thread is the 72 micro-seasons.

### 3.6 Mehtap, the crescent. Ship as the year's finale.

**Why it works.** The island changes shape with the real moon outside
your window: the strongest "the world is real" idea we have. One-stroke
drawing (One Touch Drawing) is proven, cheap and maps exactly to
constellations. The real full moon gives the arc a natural, calendar
climax.

**What is weak.** Its palette (indigo, night) sits next to Revontuli's;
the two must never be neighbours. A sleeping moon on clouds is nursery
wallpaper if it gets a face. The crescent-and-star flag risk is already
handled (stars always many).

**Fixes.** Warm it: moon silver and pale gold against Revontuli's cold
green. **No face** on the moon. Arc: wound: the moon is resting here and
is too tired to rise. Want (Nane): to stay awake for one whole night.
Turn: each constellation the player draws is a piece of a map. Bloom:
on the next real full moon after stage 4, the island glows whole and
lifts a hand's width off the clouds; the star charts join into **the
map of the outer ring**, which opens year two. This is the year's
finale; its stage 5 is the only one in the year that waits for a date
(the full moon is never more than about a month away; the wait is shown
as the moon's phase, never a countdown).

### 3.7 Çağlayan, the island that pours. Rework, then ship first.

**Why it matters.** It opens the wonder islands, it is the only free one
under cut C2, and the waterfall is the founder's own idea ("a waterfall
pouring off an island"). The first wow cannot be a 5.

**What is weak, mercilessly.** The painted concept is the template with
a waterfall on its side; nobody reading it at 60 pt sees anything but
"home with a leak". The story ends on exposition ("the water falls into
the clouds, which is why the archipelago is green"), not on anything
that happens. The route game (Pipe, Flow) is proven but brainy for a
first island.

**Fixes.** Silhouette: the second reading of section 17 (`x2-caglayan-b`),
a tall travertine column like a frozen fountain with pools spilling in
rings, is the 3; the stacked terraces are a 2. The arc in section 2 (the
fall has stopped; a leaf boat from home was blocking the spring; the fall
pours again at stage 5). The signature motion grows over the stages,
which also makes the arrival cheaper to animate. Make the route game
juicy: boats bob, splash and race through solved channels; stage 1
boards of four tiles only. The toy stays (a boat that comes back to the
home pier days later), and it now matters: a boat from home caused the
wound.

### 3.8 Çömlek, the fairy-chimney kiln. Rework, then ship ninth.

**Why it is kept.** The founder liked the mole and the wheel, and merge
(Suika) is the funniest physics in casual games: a round as a fixed lump
of clay fixes the genre's endless-round problem (minigames.md §1).

**What is weak.** The island as written is a list ("the wheel; the kiln
lit; the first pot; the shelf; the answer") ending on the same myth line
as five others. Fairy chimneys with hot-air balloons is **the** Türkiye
tourism poster: a postcard, the exact thing the contract forbids.

**Fixes.** Lead with the **kiln and the wheel**, from Avanos's red-river
clay; the balloons are not on the arrival plate at all: they rise for
the first time at stage 5, as the payoff. Arc: wound: the kiln went
cold in the loud years; Kil shapes pots that crack because nothing fires
them. Want: to make the biggest pot in the sky. Turn: a kiln needs fire
and a reason; the requests come in (Revontuli's ice to quench,
Koke's moss to pack, Çağlayan's water for the clay). Bloom: the first
firing at dawn; the chimneys glow like a lantern and the balloons rise.
It is a **convergence island** (it needs goods from four earlier
islands), so it belongs late. Never give Kil a grandparent who "left":
no loss backstory.

### 3.9 Fanus, the bell jar. Rework; a branch option.

**Why it is kept.** A world inside a glass jar is a 3 on silhouette and
a fresh idea; picross is proven (Picross S, nonogram apps with large
audiences), and wiping fogged panes is a great physical skin for it.

**What is weak, mercilessly.** Inside the glass it is a **cloud forest
in warm rain with a thumb-sized waterfall**: Koke's rain plus Çağlayan's
waterfall in a jar. It repeats two earlier islands on the sky and biome
axes. Its glass depends on the terrarium-rim shader the founder already
said does not look good (founder input, 2026-11-02). Picross is a
brainy, niche-leaning verb.

**Fixes.** Make the inside **dry and sunlit**: a Victorian conservatory
of orchids and butterflies, warm afternoon light through glass, no rain.
Picross pictures become **butterfly wings** (picross loves symmetry).
Arc: wound: the jar has been sealed for too long; the glass is fogged and
nothing inside can get out. Want (Zümrüt, the shy glass frog): to be
seen, once. Bloom: the door in the glass opens and the butterflies drift
to home's garden (the greenhouse gift). The glass must pass a **two-image
pilot** before the island is briefed; if it fails, Arı takes its slot.

### 3.10 Arı, the hanging hive (from the idea pool). Promote.

**Why.** Every island so far sits on or floats over the clouds; none
**hangs**. A great honeycomb hangs from the bough of a floating pine,
its cells little doors and windows (orientation is a contract axis).
Muğla's pine honey is real and local (Türkiye makes most of the world's
pine honey, from honeydew on Aegean pines), and bees visiting home's
sunflowers is the strongest **continuity** link any island can make.
Game: **hexa sort** (stack and merge coloured hex tiles; one of the
recent casual hits), on a honeycomb grid, juicy and distinct from the
harbor's goods sort. Palette: amber, pine green, wax cream; its own
sky: a hot, humming late afternoon.

**Risks.** A honeycomb can read as a texture, not a place: the cells
need doors, a balcony, a scale cue. Honey must stay a ritual, never a
second currency (ADR-0015). Resident: Petek, a round bumblebee who is
too heavy to fly by the textbook and does anyway. Arc: wound: the hive
is quiet; the bees will not leave it (the sky is too loud). Bloom: the
first flight to Ayçiçeği; the sunflowers turn gold.

### 3.11 Leylek. Cut as an island; keep the storks.

**Why, mercilessly.** (1) **It repeats Gezgin**: an island carried by
animals is Gezgin's idea, and Leylek will lose that comparison every
time. (2) A bundle carried by storks on ribbons reads, in most of
Europe and America, as **the stork delivering a baby**. (3) Its game,
Tiny Wings, is timing-critical, which breaks our own game rule 5 (no
timing-critical input) and VoiceOver play. (4) A seasonal island that
crosses the map for six weeks adds the most special-case code in the
system for the founder's lowest-rated concept.

**What is kept.** The storks themselves are lovely and true: white
storks cross the Bosphorus in great numbers every spring and autumn.
They become **a home event** in 1A.5's calendar: a stork pair nests on
the windmill cap each migration, every year (already Leylek's gift),
and the vapur's postcards that week come from the flyway's cities. No
island, no game, no art risk.

### 3.12 Kaplıca, Kitap, Yıldız, Ayna

- **Kaplıca (cut).** Warm water for bathing is already Revontuli's
  sauna, and travertine thermal pools are literally Pamukkale, which is
  Çağlayan. A capybara in a hot spring is the most-memed animal of the
  last years: trend, not craft. Its warm-spring ritual folds into the
  sauna and the çay ocağı.
- **Kitap (year two).** An island that is an open book with terraced
  pages passes the silhouette test. Its planned game, story ordering, is
  not a proven fun mechanic; a **jigsaw** of torn illustrated pages is
  (the most proven calm puzzle there is). Its lore role, who writes the
  bottles, makes it the right **opener of year two**.
- **Yıldız and Ayna: retired.** Mehtap and Fanus took the ideas worth
  keeping.

## 4. Order and pacing

### 4.1 The choice of two

Two moments in the year let the player choose which rumour to follow:
two sounds come from the fog in the same morning, and Pofu asks "which
one?". The chosen island arrives on the usual day; **the other arrives
next**, so nothing is lost and the choice only orders the pair. It turns
a schedule into the player's journey (the One Piece "which island next"
feeling) at no content cost.

- **Branch A (islands 3 and 4): Revontuli or Koke**, cold light or soft
  rain. Both continue the water thread (section 5) in different states.
- **Branch B (islands 7 and 8): Fanus or Arı**, glass or honey. Both
  gift home's garden, so the choice is a mood (brainy picross, juicy hexa
  sort), not a loss.

Rules: deterministic once chosen (A17); no choice is "wrong"; requests
from the second island of a pair may ask the first (it arrived earlier
for that player), so a request names a good only if the player has its
island (the backward-only rule of 1A.4 is evaluated per player, not per
release); with no answer for a day, Pofu picks the left one. Under cut
C2 both branches are Plus; the free part (home + Çağlayan) has no choice.

### 4.2 The excitement curve

Days are tended days from 1A.6. **Peak** is the intended height of the
arrival and stage-5 moment, 1–5. "Keeps" is the continuity with the step
before; "New" is the novelty axes. Texture alternates **brainy** (B),
**juicy** (J) and **rhythmic** (R).

**Why this curve has no cliffs.**

- **Peaks at 2, 5 and 10**, with 4s between; the lowest wonder step is a
  3, and never two 3s in a row whatever the player chooses (Koke, the
  quieter of branch A, sits next to Revontuli or Çanlar; Fanus next to
  Arı or Çömlek).
- **Palette families never touch**: warm-light (Çağlayan, Gezgin), cold
  (Revontuli) or wet green (Koke), bright primary (Çanlar), muted
  lamplit (Emanet), sunlit jewel (Fanus, Arı), warm-dark fire
  (Çömlek), silver blue hour (Mehtap). Revontuli and Mehtap, the two
  night islands, are six steps apart.
- **Textures alternate**: B B, then J (either branch), R, B, then a mood
  choice, J, B. The only two brainy neighbours (Çağlayan, Gezgin) are
  softened by making the route game juicy and keeping both boards small
  early.
- **Elements progress without repeating**: water (Çağlayan), living air
  (Gezgin), ice and light / rain and wood (branch A), sound (Çanlar),
  memory (Emanet), glass or honey (branch B), fire and earth (Çömlek),
  moonlight (Mehtap).
- **Each step keeps a thread from the one before**, so the novelty never
  arrives alone (rule 2).

## 5. Hand-off notes for the lore writer

**The myth, stated once:** "Islands drift toward quiet" (archipelago
§9.4). It is told a sentence per island and **answered by nobody in
year one**. Each island adds one clue; only Gezgin says the big line
aloud.

**Themes.**
- Things that stopped start again when someone is patient with them.
  No wound in Pofu is healed by force; every one heals by attention,
  which is what the player is practising in real life.
- Want and need: every resident wants something a little silly and needs
  something tender. The silly want is the comedy; the need is the arc.
- Nobody leaves. Residents gain the vapur, never a goodbye (we take
  Spiritfarer's arcs and reject its farewells).
- No villains, no death, no illness, no lost parents. Loss is about
  objects and distance, never people.
- Humour per resident: One Piece's joyful weirdness, never sarcasm.
  Tuli is serious about saunas; Yosun is late and proud; Zil feels music
  in her paws.
- Cultures are written **natively** and never as postcards (no torii,
  no Fuji, no balloon-poster Cappadocia, no Vikings).

**Recurring threads** (each pulled across islands):

| Thread | Seeded | Pulled | Paid off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The water cycle** | Çağlayan: the fall pours into the clouds | Rain on Koke, ice on Revontuli | Act one's quiet answer: the islands share one water |
| **The letters** | Day 3: the vapur's first bottle; Gezgin: undelivered letters | Koke's raked letters; Emanet's unclaimed things | Kitap (year two): who writes the bottles |
| **The song** | Day 1: Pofu hums a four-note lullaby he does not know where he learned | Gezgin's song is the same four notes; each bell on Çanlar answers it | Year two: where the lullaby comes from |
| **Pofu's own lost thing** | Day 1: the round door; one empty hook in the den | Emanet's top shelf: an object of Pofu's, with no story card | Left open in year one: the far landmark |
| **The outer ring** | The fog and its lights | Gezgin's route, Emanet's back-room map | Mehtap's star charts join into the map; year two sails there |
| **Returning allies** | Each island's feast at home | Cameos on later plates; epilogue beats | Mehtap's full-moon night: every resident on the pier |

**Mysteries to seed in the first eight days** (cheap, one line or one
prop each, never explained early): Pofu's lullaby; the empty hook in the
den; a horn that answers from the fog at dusk; the bottle's handwriting
(always the same); the whale's song matching the lullaby (day 8). Two
are paid off in year one (the song's link, the outer ring); two stay
open into year two (the bottles, Pofu's lost thing). One Piece's lesson
is that the long mystery must be **visible early and answered late**,
while every island's own story closes on time.

**Writer's checklist per island:** the wound in one picture; the want in
one sentence; the need never spoken before stage 3; a turn that
reframes, not a reveal of the myth; a stage-5 image a child would draw;
a feast line for Pofu; one thread to a later island; a seasonal beat;
nothing that names a date, a deadline or a loss of a person.

## 6. Self-critique

- **Peggle carries casino DNA.** Its ancestor is pachinko. The fixes
  (no multipliers, no fever slot, deterministic yield) must survive
  tuning, or Revontuli goes back to the drawing board rather than ship a
  slot machine in an anti-doomscroll app.
- **Branching doubles the release pairs.** A season must ship both
  islands of a pair or neither; if one slips, the choice is skipped that
  year, which is fine but must be designed for.
- **Arcs cost writing.** Each island now needs a want, a need and a
  turn: about 40 lines instead of 30. Cheap next to art, but a native
  writer per culture is still the bottleneck.
- **The growing signature motion** (a trickle that becomes a fall) makes
  arrival cheaper but delays the wow by days; the arrival still needs
  one unmistakable image (the column, the whale's eye), or rule 1 costs
  us the first impression.
- **Cutting Leylek and Kaplıca removes two culturally Turkish-adjacent
  ideas**; Arı and Çömlek keep Türkiye present in the sky, and the
  storks stay at home.
- **Scores are judgement**, calibrated on the founder's ratings; the
  silhouette pilots (Çağlayan b, Emanet's umbrella, Fanus's glass,
  Revontuli's floe beside the loved painting) are the real test.
