## 3. Contradictions and possibly-wrong facts

- **C1 (doc vs his words). home-progression "Founder decisions (2026-11-05)" misstates him.** It quotes "seasons follow the real calendar plus a weekly small theme; residents first get a fixed favourite station (moving later)". His words: "mevsimler takvime bağlı olabilir haftalık da olabilir bilmiyorum taşınma olayından tam emin değilim değerlendirelim" (FM 11-05 15:16). Neither is a decision, and "taşınma" means a story character moving to home (his next message, 16:24), not reassigning residents between stations.
- **C2 (doc vs doc).** home-progression §2/§6: "1 more [resident] per main island … moves in"; home-layout header and §11.5: they do **not** move in, they visit ("superseded"). home-progression was never updated.
- **C3 (doc internal).** home-progression header says moving residents opens at IL4; §6 says "can be moved at any time for free"; §13.3 recommends free moves. Code has no assignment at all.
- **C4 (code vs map).** `VisitorSchedule.standardRoster` comment: "The seven main islands in story order" lists koke, caglayan, emanet, gezgin, revontuli, ari, mehtap. Map: Koke is a filler, Arı a home upgrade, Gezgin the vehicle; map order puts Revontuli before Emanet. Thursday/Yosun is not on the map (book-one.md §6 notes it). Wednesday on the map is "kapıda bir paket", not Boncuk visiting.
- **C5 (doc vs code).** home-layout §2/§11: harbor moves into the home frame (islet at x 295…495, bridge). Code: `IslandLayout.swift:160` still `Isle(id: .harbor, center: (265, 320))`. MAP's "Evin varacağı hal" image (`B-img/map-home-acilis.png`) shows İskele attached at the right by a bridge, lighthouse beam on. Today İskele never shows at Home (`B-img/home-growth-il1-il10.jpg`).
- **C6 (doc/request vs app). "Every level a living, visible change" is not true on screen.** The IL1-10 sheet at the Home framing (`B-img/home-growth-il1-il10.jpg`) shows IL1-IL5 almost identical (a pond appears), IL8-IL10 nearly indistinguishable, no harbor, no Tepe ceremony look; the whole-island sheet (`B-img/home-whole-il1-il10.jpg`) shows the island as a small sliver. Tracker 2.6 "partly: art for most steps (#235)" overstates it: **all 16 growth pieces are code-drawn stand-ins** (`GrowthArt.swift`; no `growth-*` imageset baked; batch-65 #306 merged **manifests only**).
- **C7 (ADR vs his words, art QA vs its own gate).** ADR-0018 §5 makes "the less contrasty island take" the style target; he said the haze "hep fazla gelmişti" (11-03) and objects are pale (11-05 twice). - **C9 (doc vs doc, visual).** island-undersides §5: Çağlayan's healed falls pour off the underside as "a long fall below the plate". home-layout §12.5 recommends a short fall into its own pool because the long fall "read as scary".
