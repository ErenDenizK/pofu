# Apple Design Awards: what the app winners teach Pofu

_Last updated: 2026-11-05. Scope: apps only (not games), ADA 2023 to 2026, plus
wellbeing and habit apps near the awards. Overlap with
[`competitive-analysis.md`](competitive-analysis.md) (Unrot, Brainrot, pricing,
Finch retention) is skipped; this note is about craft, not market position._

## Method and confidence

Reasons below are quoted or closely paraphrased from Apple's own award pages
([2023](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2023/),
[2024](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2024/),
[2025](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/),
[2026](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/)), read 2026-11-05. Apple
publishes descriptions for all 2025 and 2026 winners and finalists, but for
2023 and 2024 only for winners; finalists there have no text. "Technique" is
our reading of Apple's text (marked **(ours)** where it goes beyond it).
Finch, one sec and Clearspace are not in the 2023 to 2026 lists; Halide
(2022), CARROT Weather (2021) and Things (2017) won earlier and are only
background.

## What wins

1. **One simple idea, finished.** CapWords ("learn the name of a thing by
   taking a picture of it"), grug ("nothing extraneous"), Moonlitt. Pofu's
   sentence: resist an open, your island grows.
2. **Sensory detail on every transition.** CapWords pairs each flash card with
   a real-world sound; Denim has custom haptics; Opal has haptics on "gems";
   Vocabulary has haptics. Polish is cumulative, not one payoff.
3. **Accessibility as a core design goal.** oko (haptic and audio), Guitar Wiz
   (VoiceOver tells you where fingers go), Speechify (reduce cognitive load).
   All name Dynamic Type and VoiceOver explicitly; Guitar Wiz adds Increased
   Contrast and Differentiate Without Color.
4. **Cut decisions.** Structured's pre-filled tasks, Speechify's low cognitive
   load, Moonlitt's easy onboarding.
5. **Gentle beats insistent.** Gentler Streak is praised for "not insistent
   reminders" and for progress over comparison; The Outsiders balances
   recovery; Structured values downtime. This is ADR-0017.
6. **Calm at a charged moment.** Primary refuses clickbait; Watch Duty is
   clear and reliable. Our shield appears at the weakest moment.
7. **A character that carries state.** Duolingo's mascot, Bears Gratitude's
   bears as a way into the habit, Harvee's guy as "an avatar for a person's
   state". Not guilt, but a face for the data.
8. **Voice and tone are design material.** grug's grunts and release notes,
   Evolve's inclusive tone, Duolingo's tenacious mascot.
9. **Live and glanceable surfaces.** Flighty's Live Activities, Lumy's
   widgets, Tide Guide's widgets that "save a step", Headspace's Watch
   moment, Opal's widgets and Shortcuts.
10. **Time-aware visuals.** Tide Guide's sky-matched palette, Mela dimming
    steps at the right time, Flighty's airport look. Context drives the UI.
11. **Familiar grammar, hidden depth.** Procreate Dreams reuses gestures,
    Play is approachable, (Not Boring) has pro features under big buttons.
12. **Platform depth.** Liquid Glass, Foundation Models on device (Structured,
    Hearing Buddy, Harvee), Dynamic Island, Live Activities, Watch.

## For Pofu

P1 do soon, P2 next, P3 later. Effort: S under 2 days, M under 2 weeks, L
longer. All respect ADR-0015 (seeds never buy screen time) and ADR-0017.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | app | **Haptic and sound palette (P1).** 4 to 5 named haptics (nudge, resist, seed gain, growth, quiet) plus sound; a sound on every shield transition, as CapWords does. Document in `docs/brand/`. | S | 2, 3 |
| 2 | app | **Calm shield copy rules (P1).** One line plus one action; no shame, no sensationalism (Primary); add a tone ladder to `pofu-voice.md`. | S | 5, 6 |

| 5 | app | **Accessibility pass as a deliverable (P1).** VoiceOver text that describes the island scene and shield in words (Guitar Wiz), Dynamic Type to largest, Increased Contrast, Differentiate Without Color, reduce-motion variants, haptics-only path. | M | 3 |
| 6 | app | **Onboarding that drafts, user confirms (P2).** Targets Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and a schedule pre-selected; one calm Screen Time authorisation screen with a declined fallback (Structured, Moonlitt). | M | 4 |

| 11 | game | **Progressive depth (P2).** First visit shows one action; decorations and residents unlock by visit count. | M | 11 |
| 12 | app | **Hand-voiced details (P3).** Release notes, empty states and the status copy written in Pofu's voice (grug). Use the pofu-voice skill. | S | 8 |

Order: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, then 6 to 11, then 12 to 15. Opal being a 2025 Social
Impact finalist shows screen-time wellbeing is in scope for the awards, and
its "gems" haptics are the closest precedent for idea 1.

## What not to take

- **Spatial computing and 3D.** Taobao, CellWalk, Caradise, Metaballs, NBA
  are Vision Pro showcases; off-roadmap for a 1 to 2 minute iPhone visit.
- **Mascot guilt and decay.** Duolingo-style pressure conflicts with ADR-0017.
  Take the reactive face, not the threat.
- **Leaderboards and competitive quests** (Duolingo). They reward volume; we
  want fewer opens.
- **On-device AI for its own sake.** Structured and Harvee use it to cut
  decisions or protect privacy. Pofu has few decisions to cut.
- **"Vibes" at the cost of function.** (Not Boring) works because the
  products "excel in all other areas too"; the shield must stay fast.
- **Chasing the award.** Winners are judged on finished craft; the dogfood
  week comes first.
- **Over-trusting this note.** 2023 and 2024 finalists have no Apple text, so
  nothing here is claimed about them; revisit when Apple adds it.
