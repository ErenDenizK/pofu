# Apple Design Awards: what the app winners teach Pofu

_Last updated: 2026-11-05. Scope: apps only (not games), ADA 2023 to 2026, plus
wellbeing and habit apps near the awards. Overlap with
[`competitive-analysis.md`](competitive-analysis.md) (Unrot, Brainrot, Opal
pricing, one sec, Finch retention) is deliberately skipped; this note is about
craft, not market position._

## Method and confidence

- `apple.com`, `developer.apple.com` and the news sites were not reachable
  from the cloud session (host not allowed), so no Apple page was read in
  full. Winner and finalist lists come from search results that quote Apple,
  MacRumors, 9to5Mac, Gear Patrol and the apps' own blogs.
- Each row is tagged **cited** (a source quoted the reason), **reported**
  (reviewer or secondary wording), or **inferred** (our reading, not a claim
  about what Apple said). Techniques are mostly **inferred**: Apple's
  citations are one or two sentences. Verify the inferred ones on a device
  before building.
- Finalist lists for some categories (2025 Interaction, Inclusivity, Visuals;
  2026 Social Impact) could not be mapped reliably and are omitted, not
  guessed. The 2026 sources disagree on whether Social Impact existed.
- Apple's own criteria, per the category pages: Delight and Fun = memorable,
  engaging, satisfying and enhanced by Apple technology; Social Impact =
  improves lives and shines a light on crucial issues.

## What wins

1. **One idea, expressed fully.** CapWords is "photo to sticker to word";
   oko is "signal to haptic". The pitch fits one sentence and every screen
   serves it. Pofu's sentence: "when you resist an open, your island grows."
2. **A payoff moment crafted to the frame.** The reaction to the core action
   (CapWords' sticker animation, Duolingo's mascot) is the product. For Pofu
   the payoff is the resist moment on the shield and the first frame back
   on the island.
3. **Accessibility is design, not compliance.** oko works through haptics and
   audio; Speechify is praised for VoiceOver and simplicity. Inclusivity
   finalists in 2026 (Structured, Hearing Buddy) cut decisions or add a
   sense, rather than adding settings.
4. **Reduce decisions for stressed or neurodivergent users.** Structured's
   suggest-and-pre-fill, Play's simple first surface. The user confirms;
   the app does the work.
5. **Calm under stress or temptation.** Watch Duty and Primary win by being
   plain and non-sensational. Our shield appears at the exact moment a user
   is weakest; calm beats clever there.
6. **Gentle systems beat punishing ones.** Gentler Streak is cited for
   lifestyle improvement for everyone and is described as gentle. This
   matches our ADR-0017 (Pofu never dies).
7. **Live, glanceable surfaces.** Flighty and Headspace use the wrist, Live
   Activity and Dynamic Island so the app is useful without being opened.
   For a blocker, the widget and Live Activity are the product between
   opens.
8. **Haptics and sound as design materials.** oko makes them a primary
   channel. Winners tune them, not just enable them (inferred for most;
   cited for oko).
9. **Depth hidden behind a simple first surface.** Play, Procreate Dreams.
   Progressive disclosure: the novice sees one thing, the expert finds more.
10. **Character with a point of view.** Duolingo's mascot and CARROT's voice
    (CARROT 2021) show that personality in copy and animation is rewarded
    when it never blocks the task.
11. **Restraint and long-term craft.** iA Writer is a finalist after 15 years
    by removing things. Apple rewards what is left out.
12. **Apple-technology depth.** Delight and Fun criteria say "enhanced by
    Apple technologies": on-device models (Structured), camera (oko,
    CapWords), widgets, Live Activities, Watch. Using the platform well is
    part of the score.

## For Pofu

Priorities: P1 do soon, P2 next, P3 later. Effort: S under 2 days, M under
2 weeks, L longer. Everything here respects ADR-0015 (seeds never buy screen
time) and ADR-0017 (Pofu never dies).

| # | Tag | Idea | Effort | Why (principle) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | app | **Shield resist moment as a signature beat.** On "not now", a 1-second Pofu reaction plus a distinct haptic pattern, then a clean dismissal. One reaction per outcome (resist, open anyway). | M | 2, 8 |
| 2 | app | **Haptic and sound palette.** Define 4 to 5 named haptics (nudge, resist, seed gain, growth, quiet) and a matching mute-friendly sound set; document in `docs/brand/`. Test with VoiceOver and reduce-motion on. | S | 3, 8 |
| 3 | app | **Calm shield copy rules.** Never shame, never sensational, one line plus one action; add a "tone ladder" to `pofu-voice.md` checked by tests for max length. | S | 5, 6 |
| 4 | app | **Live Activity and widget as the between-opens product.** A "today's resists" widget and a Live Activity during an active shield window; glance answer is Pofu mood plus count. | M | 7 |
| 5 | app | **Accessibility pass as a design deliverable.** VoiceOver labels on the island and shield, Dynamic Type to the largest sizes, reduce-motion variants of every reaction, haptics-only feedback path. Aim for an Inclusivity-worthy story. | M | 3 |
| 6 | app | **Onboarding that drafts for the user.** Pre-select Instagram, YouTube, TikTok targets and a default schedule; the user only confirms (Structured pattern). Screen Time authorisation explained in one calm screen with a fallback if declined. | M | 4, 9 |
| 7 | game | **A one-second payoff on seed gain.** Seed pops, lands in the counter, island nudges; same duration every time so it never feels slow across 1 to 2 minute visits. | S | 2 |
| 8 | game | **Forgiving streaks.** Missed day pauses a "rest" leaf instead of resetting; copy says rest, not failure. Align with Gentler Streak. Needs a game-design note before build. | S | 6 |
| 9 | game | **Pofu reacts to real state.** Mood and idle animation follow today's resist ratio and time of day (Duolingo-style mascot as state display, without guilt). | M | 10 |
| 10 | game | **Progressive depth on the island.** First visit shows one action; decoration, residents and quests unlock by visit count so the first minute is never busy. | M | 9 |
| 11 | app | **Watch glance (later).** A complication showing today's resists; Headspace and Flighty show a wrist surface can carry a wellbeing product. | L | 7 |
| 12 | game | **Camera or on-device trick for delight (later).** A CapWords-style "scan something real, Pofu reacts" moment as an optional earn path; evaluate against the verification question in the competitive note. | L | 12 |
| 13 | app | **Awards framing.** Opal was a 2025 Social Impact finalist, so screen-time wellbeing is in scope. Keep a short "why this deserves an award" note (one idea, calm, accessible, gentle) to guide reviews. | S | 1, 11 |

Suggested order: 2, 3, 7, 1, 5, 6, 4, 8, 9, 10, 13, then 11 and 12.

## What not to take

- **Copying winners' surfaces.** Taobao is a dense commerce interface; its
  award rewards interaction craft at that density, not a layout to imitate.
- **Mascot guilt.** Duolingo-style pressure and Brainrot-style decay conflict
  with ADR-0017. Take the reactive character, not the threat.
- **Spatial computing, 3D and AR.** Feather, Primary and Moonlitt show
  platform reach, but Vision Pro or heavy 3D is off the roadmap for a 1 to 2
  minute iPhone visit.
- **AI features for their own sake.** Structured's on-device suggestions work
  because they cut decisions. Pofu has few decisions to cut; do not add a
  model to look current.
- **Chasing the award.** Apple judges finished craft over years (iA Writer,
  15 years). We have a dogfood week to run first; do not reorder the roadmap
  around a category.
- **Unverified claims.** Rows marked inferred, and anything marked "reasons
  not found", are not evidence. Re-check with Apple's pages when the host
  becomes reachable.
