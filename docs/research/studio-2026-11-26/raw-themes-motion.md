## 0. False premise in the brief
The brief says "in-game season system (NOT real-world seasons)". I found no doc that plans an in-game season cycle with a start and an end. Every look-season doc says the real calendar (see 1a). The word "season" is also used for a release cycle (see 1c). No doc, ADR or note describes how an in-game season starts or ends.
