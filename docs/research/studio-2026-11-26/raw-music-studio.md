## 2. Making game music with AI and agents (web, 2026-11-26)

**A. Adaptive structures (secondary only; vendor docs UNCHECKED).**
- Vertical layering: layers fade in/out with game state; horizontal resequencing: music cut into short segments that loop, branch or transition; stingers: short hits on events, sometimes beat/phrase-locked; hybrids exist (Sackboy: horizontal segments + a vertical success layer). Sources: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/horizontal-resequencing-and-dynamic-transitions-for-game-music-composers , https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/hybrid-horizontal-vertical-structure-for-game-music-composers-from-spyder-to-sackboy-gdc-2021- , https://www.audiokinetic.com/en/blog/how-to-use-interactive-music-to-score-gameplay/ , https://online.berklee.edu/takenote/?p=14985 (search snippets, pages not fully read).
- In this repo the same ideas already exist for ambience: layered beds by state (AmbienceMixer) and ducking; sound-identity.md §4.2 designs calm to voicing/filter layers.

**B. LLM composition as notation/MIDI + rendering.**
- ChatMusician (open LLM trained on ABC notation; MIT per its model card): ABC uses about 38% of MIDI tokens (288 tokens/song); model card renders ABC with the `symusic` library; notes direct generation may score worse. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16153 , https://huggingface.co/m-a-p/ChatMusician (snippets, not fully read).
- GeneralUser GS SoundFont licence (primary, raw LICENSE.txt): "You may use GeneralUser GS without restriction for your own music creation, private or commercial"; "feel free to use it in your software projects, and to modify the SoundFont bank or its packaging"; no explicit general redistribution clause (only asks not to hotlink downloads); author says some samples may be of uncertain origin. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS/main/documentation/LICENSE.txt
- Apple `AVAudioUnitSampler` / `AVAudioSequencer`: my search summaries say Sampler loads SF2 and DLS via `loadSoundBankInstrument`, Sequencer loads Standard MIDI Files; Apple doc pages returned only titles. UNCHECKED against Apple. A developer-forum thread reports SF2 bank load failures tied to bankMSB/LSB mapping (https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/60265, snippet). FluidSynth licence and other renderers: UNCHECKED.
- Offline rendering already exists in-repo: `tools/sound/render.py` synthesizes from numbers (numpy/scipy, ffmpeg), measures LUFS/true peak.
