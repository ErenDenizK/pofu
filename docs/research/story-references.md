# Story references: Genshin Impact, Zelda, Naruto

**Date:** 2026-11-05. **Asked by:** the founder (take their spirit, never
copy one-to-one). Three Opus research passes, mostly from knowledge;
uncertain facts are marked in the passes and left out here. Read this
before researching these works again.

## What each work teaches Pofu

### Genshin Impact
- **A voice for a silent player.** The Traveler barely speaks; Paimon
  reacts, sums up and names people. Pofu: the player never speaks (fits
  the secret Bekçi); Pofu and Mochi talk as a pair, about the player's
  quiet, never to the player. Say less than Paimon (her dialogue is the
  game's most criticised part).
- **Quest types you tell apart at a glance** (Archon, Story, World,
  Event). Pofu: a main-story island always arrives the same way (Gezgin,
  one signal, a small ceremony); a filler just drifts in on a quiet day.
- **One place, one ideal, one ruler** (Freedom, Contract, Eternity). Pofu:
  each main island has one theme of quiet and one grand resident who
  embodies it.
- **The god first met as an ordinary person** (Venti a bard, Zhongli an
  undertaker). Pofu: a grand resident first looks small; the grandeur is
  revealed later, so it feels earned.
- **Separate seasonal islands** (Golden Apple Archipelago, 1.6 and 2.8).
  Pofu: exactly the filler model, minus the deadline.
- **A home friends visit and residents move into** (Serenitea Pot).
  Pofu: met residents visit Ayçiçeği; Gezgin carries you to friends'
  harbors.
- **Do not take:** gacha, timed FOMO events, daily chores and energy caps.

### Zelda: Breath of the Wild, Tears of the Kingdom, Wind Waker
- **A giant animal you stand on** (Divine Beasts). Pofu: Gezgin's back is
  a small place that gains one detail each time the main story moves.
- **The creature that was in plain sight all along** (the Light Dragon is
  Zelda). Pofu: the grown, godlike Nazlı is not a new creature; her early
  form carries hints from day one (a lantern hook on her back, a scar
  shaped like a switched-off light), so the reveal reads "she was always
  this".
- **Memories found at places, in any order.** Pofu: each main island holds
  one object of the old Bekçi; each adds one sentence, written so the
  order can flex.
- **The region heals when its main step ends** (the rain stops at Zora's
  Domain). Pofu: each main island leaves one small, permanent change at
  home; a filler leaves only a keepsake.
- **A freed beast stays on the horizon.** Pofu: finished main islands
  keep a faint light on the horizon, seen from the harbor: progress
  without a meter.
- **Grandeur from scale and arrival, not words.** Pofu: a main resident
  gets a staged arrival (fog, scale against Pofu, one gesture) and one or
  two lines.
- **The talking boat that is a king** (King of Red Lions). Pofu: crossings
  on Gezgin are where Nazlı says her one remembered line, one per trip.
- **Do not take:** collectible checklists (900 Korok seeds), padding hunts
  (the Triforce chart), pressure systems (Blood Moon, breaking weapons).
- **Lesson:** design the final picture first, then plant its pieces from
  week one as quiet, unexplained details.

### Naruto and Shippuden
- **A giant's respect is earned** (Gamabunta scorns Naruto until he holds
  on). Pofu: early Nazlı does not obey; she drifts where it is quiet;
  Pofu earns her trust by staying still on her back, which is his own
  lesson.
- **Hostility to partnership** (Kurama gives his name, the fist-bump).
  Pofu: Nazlı's grown form arrives because she chooses partnership, marked
  by one earned gesture: her real name, or the song she never sang.
- **A level-up you read in one look** (Sage Mode's eye markings). Pofu:
  one visible change per milestone (Gezgin's island blooms, the harbor
  lantern changes colour), never numbers.
- **The Will of Fire, a job handed on.** Pofu: the Bekçi is not a
  bloodline but an act; the reveal names what the player has been doing
  all along.
- **The mentor seen through what he left** (Jiraiya's book). Pofu: the old
  Bekçi is an empty chair at Mochi's stall, a half-finished lullaby;
  Mochi finally sitting in that chair is her turn.
- **Rivals who complete each other** (Naruto and Sasuke). Pofu: Pofu
  restless, Mochi terse; each holds the other's missing lesson.
- **One supporting character, one defining moment** (Haku, Rock Lee,
  Iruka). Pofu: every grand resident gets one striking design idea and one
  act the player remembers.
- **A giant creature that is an island** (the Island Turtle). Pofu: some
  islands turn out to be sleeping creatures, which foreshadows Nazlı.
- **Good fillers are small and self-contained** (a joke or a feeling, no
  new powers or lore, never at a cliffhanger, never many in a row).
- **Do not take:** violence as the climax, endless power escalation (the
  grown Nazlı is the ceiling, reached once a book), padding.

## Synthesis: the story rules these give Pofu

1. **Design backwards.** The final night is fixed first: at Mehtap the
   dark stars join into the trail and it ends at home; Nazlı arrives
   grown and sings the four notes; Mochi sits; the player is named the
   Bekçi once. Everything before plants a piece of that picture.
2. **A main island** = one theme of quiet + one grand resident who first
   looks small + one object of the old Bekçi + one permanent change at
   home + a light left on the horizon. It always arrives on Gezgin.
3. **A filler** = drifts in on a random quiet day, complete in one or two
   visits, a joke or a feeling, no lore, no counter, never expires, never
   lands right before a story beat. The duo bickers.
4. **Gezgin** grows with the bond, not with the player's spending: humble
   and wilful at first, a detail added to her back per main island,
   hints of her grown form in plain sight, the grown form chosen once at
   the end of the book. Each book can bring a new edition of her.
5. **The duo** speaks for the silent player, briefly, and each teaches the
   other something neither admits.
6. **No fixed days.** Order and gates only (the previous main island's
   bloom plus quiet days); the numbers come from tests.
