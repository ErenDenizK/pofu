## 1. How islands grow, and how the stations on them grow

### 1.1 Vocabulary, as written and as coded

- **Island level (IL, "Ada seviyesi", 1 to 10).** One number for the home cluster. Rises when a tended-day floor AND a growth threshold are both met; never falls. Growth = sum of built station levels ("büyüme"). Each IL raises the station cap and opens slots. src home-progression.md:86-158 (§3), PC/Game/Archipelago.swift:323-337. 2026-11-05, L (progression designer agent). CURRENT (numbers below).
- **Station.** A build slot with five levels. Eight in Book One: garden (Bahçe), tea stall (Çay ocağı), pond (Gölet), workshop (Atölye), windmill (Değirmen), bagel cart (Simit arabası), pier (İskele/Rıhtım), lighthouse (Fener). Five on home, three on the harbor; a ninth, the hive (Arı), is a gift station from IL9. src home-progression.md:69-85; home-layout.md:76-90 (slots S1-S9 with world coordinates and the IL that opens each); PC/Game/Archipelago.swift:55-62, 345-352. CURRENT in code (eight stations; hive not in `ArchipelagoCatalog.standard`).
- **Station level (L1-L5, "Seviye n / 5").** Costs per level in seeds, e.g. garden 0/5/9/15/25, tea stall 8/14/24/38/60 (1,950 seeds for all eight at L5). src home-progression.md:140-157; PC/Game/Archipelago.swift:345-352. CURRENT.
- **Mastery levels L6-L10** (8 stations x 5, "a small touch per level, a golden master at L10", cap rises one a season). src home-progression.md:79, 240, 341 (§9). 2026-11-05, L. UNCLEAR/not built: the code's `stationCap` never exceeds 5 (Archipelago.swift:325-334).
- **Stage (0-4) of a wonder island** (Koke, Çağlayan, later Revontuli...): a healing step, one at most per day, on a resisted day. 0 stuck, 4 healed. src island-healing-arc.md:13-22; PC/Game/WonderIslands.swift:143 (`healedStage = 4`). CURRENT. Not the same axis as IL.
- **Plate "stage" index in code** for Home: ten painted day plates, one per island level, stored newest first: `stageLevels [10,9,...,1]`, so stage 0 = IL10. src PW/Resources/Plates/plate-home.json (`stages 10`, `stageLevels`); PW/Plate/PlateIsland.swift:268-271 (`dayStage(islandLevel:)`). CURRENT.
- **Names for the top of growth:** Tam Çiçek (full bloom, IL10), Tepe (the hilltop above the windmill, opens at IL10), Altın Ada (all eight at mastery L10, "the rim and lip turn gold at dusk"). src home-progression.md:223-240; concept-flows.md:191-193; story map p1 "Ev büyür: ... → Tam Çiçek → Tepe → Altın Ada" (book-one.md:93-95). CURRENT as plan; only IL1-IL10 exist in code.
