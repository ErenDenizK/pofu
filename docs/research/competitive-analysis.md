# Competitive Analysis

_Last updated: 2026-10-31. Sources are listed at the end. Third-party pricing
data varies by source and region; treat numbers as indicative._

## Primary references

The two products the founder identified as direct references. Observations
come from their App Store screenshot sets (captured from the Turkish App
Store) and public listings.

### Unrot: Earn your Screen Time (Unrot OÜ)

**Mechanic:** earn coins through real-life activities, spend coins to unblock
apps for N minutes.

Observed features:

- Home: a pink brain mascot in a sunny meadow, a "Brain Coins" progress bar
  (80/100), and two primary actions: _Unrot_ (earn) and _Scroll_ (spend).
- "Get rich quick" quick actions: Breathe, Journal, Exercise, Side Quests.
- **Unrots catalog:** activities with hourly earn rates (Clean 15/hr,
  Practice Instrument 20/hr, Study 20/hr, Workout 15/hr), filterable by
  category (Focus, Exercise, Social, Creative), favoritable, extensible with
  a "+" button.
- **Screentime Store:** a playful "Unrot Bank" card with the mascot, a grid of
  unlock durations priced 1 coin per minute (1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15 min), and a
  "Buy Screentime" CTA.
- Mini-tools: Unrot Timer, Exercises, Breathing, Side Quests, Journaling,
  Earned Scrolling.
- Day/night scene variants ("What are we doing in the real world?").

Strengths: the earn-and-spend economy is an intuitive, positive reframe of
blocking. Clean, friendly visual system.

Weaknesses:

- **Honor system:** activities are timer-based self-reports; trivially gamed.
- **Mandatory subscription with no free tier.** Review sentiment analysis
  attributes roughly 30% negative sentiment, mostly to pricing and the lack of
  a trial.
- No social layer. Flat 1 coin = 1 minute economy with no inflation control.

### Brainrot: Screen Time Control (Smolworks Inc.)

**Mechanic:** a brain mascot's health percentage decays as you use
"brainrot apps".

Observed features:

- Home: the mascot in a world whose state reflects health (lush green at 96%,
  barren grey at 60%), a health bar, tiles for Screen Time, Pickups, and Focus
  Time, and a "Top Offenders" list of the most-used apps.
- **Block:** active blockers ("No BS, All Day"), app lists ("Brainrot Apps"),
  and illustrated presets ("Touch Grass: Reconnect with reality", "Ready for
  Gym: Get them reps in").
- **Focus mode:** a zen garden scene with the meditating mascot and a timer;
  mirrored in a Live Activity ("Keep Going!").
- **Widgets:** a "Start Focus" widget.
- **Streaks:** a "Days Streak" screen with a weekday tracker, "Challenges and
  Rewards" milestones (7 days, 14 days), and themes unlocked by streaks.
- **Intervention screen:** "Do you REALLY need to use TikTok right now?" with
  "Nah, you're right" / "Yes I really do".
- **Shield screen:** "Hydrate, instead of doomscrolling. Drink some water,
  stretch. Instagram can wait an hour." with an "Ask my brain" action.
- Five-tab navigation: Home, Block, Focus (center), Stats, Settings.

Strengths: the strongest emotional hook in the category (the guilt of hurting
the cute brain, cited in user reviews). Polished widget and Live Activity
work.

Weaknesses:

- All features described in the listing require a subscription.
- No earn mechanic: the only lever is "use less", which offers no positive
  alternative.
- No social layer.

## Synthesis: where the opening is

1. **Nobody combines the two halves.** Unrot has the positive earn loop;
   Brainrot has the emotional decay loop. Together they form a complete
   behavioral system: a reason to stop (the companion wilts) and a reason to
   do something else (you earn energy).
2. **Nobody is social.** Accountability partners are among the most effective
   interventions for behavior change, and the founder's initial audience is a
   friend group.
3. **Nobody is fair on price outside the US.** Every reference app paywalls
   the core. A free core with paid delight is both differentiated and
   aligned with word-of-mouth growth.
4. **Verification is either absent (Unrot) or the entire product (Pushscroll).**
   A trust-first model with verification bonuses sits in the unoccupied
   middle.
5. **Character craft is the real competitive bar.** The relevant benchmark is
   not other blockers but Finch and Duolingo: products whose characters people
   genuinely care about.
