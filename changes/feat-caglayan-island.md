Added: Çağlayan, the second wonder island, is playable end to end: a rumour at the harbor once Koke is healed (the water noise stopped somewhere), the island two days later, and four stages, each earned by finishing that stage's flow round (two boards) on a day with a resisted reach, at most one stage a day. Damla stands by the pool, the mill hut keeps the old keeper's note, Mochi reads the wall at the turn, the healed waterfall pours off the island's underside, and the pool's fountain flows at home from then on (Damla visits on Tuesdays).
