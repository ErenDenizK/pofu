# Banned AI tics (Turkish and English)

A hit is a prompt to look again, not an automatic ban, except where
marked **never**. Sources: narrative-craft §3.6 (Turkish starter list),
the deslop and humanizer skills in show-me-the-story, stop-slop,
humanizer, the EQ-Bench slop list, and our own drafts. Extend it from
every critic round and every panel comment that says "sounds like AI".

## Turkish: words and phrases

| Tic | Why | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| büyülü, sihirli, masalsı, rüya gibi | Names wonder instead of showing it | The thing itself |
| huzur, huzurlu, dinginlik, sükûnet | Our theme said aloud; **never** in a line | An image of quiet (a frog, once) |
| yolculuk, macera, keşfet, serüven | Brochure Turkish | What they do |
| sessizlik, sessiz (in dialogue) | The theme word; ration to one per island | A sound stopping |
| kalbi ısındı, içi ısındı, kalbinin sesi | Cliché | Body: cheeks, quills, fluff |
| derin bir nefes aldı, gözleri parladı, hafif bir gülümseme, gözleri doldu | The Turkish twin of "took a deep breath" | A specific action or nothing |
| aslında, sanki, bir şekilde, adeta | Hedges an AI uses to sound thoughtful | Cut |
| unutma ki, bazen … gerekir, önemli olan … | Aphorism and lecture; **never** from Pofu | Cut |
| an'ın tadını çıkar, anı yaşa, kendine iyi bak | Wellness copy; **never** | Cut |
| sadece X değil, aynı zamanda Y; X değil, Y | The "not X but Y" structure | Say Y |
| Bu, … (as a pronoun opening a sentence) | Calque of "This is…" | "Ne güzel." |
| sahip olmak for everything | Calque of "have" | "… var" |
| -ebilir misin / -ebilir miyim as politeness | Calque of "can you" | "Yardım edeyim mi?" |
| kesinlikle, gerçekten çok, inanılmaz | English emphasis | Word order, "ya" |
| kim bilir…, belki de… (as a scene closer) | Fake mystery | End on the object |
| meğer (more than once per island) | The reveal tic | Show the reveal |

## Turkish: structures

- **Small mistake, deadpan correction** ("Biri musluğu açık unutmuş." →
  "...Musluk değilmiş."). Good once; draft 0 used it three times in five
  scenes. Budget: one per island.
- A line **explaining the image it just made** ("Öyle bir sessizlik.").
- **"…ya."** ending more than one line in three; **"değil mi?"** tags.
- **Ellipsis openings** ("...") more than one per scene.
- **Three-beat lists** of nouns or adjectives in a line or a stage
  direction.
- Every speaker answering in **two-word fragments**: terseness is also a
  tic. Let one line breathe per scene.
- A character **summarising the arc** in the last line ("Doldu ya." risks
  this: the theme in a word).

## English (glosses, and the later English pass)

- delve, tapestry, testament, embark, journey, whisper of, gentle
  reminder, a sense of [wonder / belonging / peace], something shifted,
  for the first time, and that was enough, the weight of, softly,
  quietly (as a tag), a small smile, eyes sparkled, heart swelled
- "voice barely a whisper", "took a deep breath", "couldn't help but",
  "a smile playing on her lips", "the air was thick with" (EQ-Bench
  n-grams)
- "It's not X, it's Y"; "not just X, but Y"; negative lists ("Not a…
  Not a… A…"); one-line closers that sound like pull quotes
- Em-dash chains; adverbial tags ("she said softly"); said-bookisms

## Pofu-specific never-say (from pofu-voice §4.5, lore bible §6.11)

Phones, screens, feeds, apps, the algorithm, "digital", "detox",
"mindful", health claims, guilt ("beni bıraktın"), shame, statistics,
"you should", death, illness, lost parents, seeds as a price for screen
time, the fog as a villain.
