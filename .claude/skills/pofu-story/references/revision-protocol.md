# Revision protocol

Two modes.

- **Light (default).** Write the scene naturally (SKILL.md), then do
  sections 4 to 6 below as quick checks: fix the lines that name a
  feeling, explain the lore or explain a joke; trim what the art shows
  anyway; read it aloud. No quotas, no card. Add the critic (section 7)
  for key beats.
- **Full (when stuck, or a scene keeps coming out flat).** Every role in
  order, one job each. Round 1 showed the full mode pushes every line
  toward two words; use it to find the scene, then rewrite the result
  naturally.

When sub-agents are available, run the **critic** in a fresh context
with only the scene and the two checklists: a critic that remembers
writing the scene defends it.

## 0. Card

```
SCENE:      <id, place, time, who is on screen>
JOB:        <one sentence: what changes between the first and last beat>
WANT/NEED:  <speaker: want said aloud / need never said>
TACTICS:    <speaker: what they are trying to do to the other, as a verb:
             deflect, test, give, refuse, tease, stall>
FORBIDDEN:  <the honest line each speaker would say; banned in any form>
OBJECT:     <the one thing that carries the feeling>
DEVICE A/B: <primary device per variant; see the list below>
LAST SCENE: <device used last time for these characters; do not repeat>
CALLBACKS:  <habits or lines from earlier scenes to echo, with change>
CAP:        <max spoken words; default 40>
ENDING:     <object or line; never a hook, the theme word or a moral>
```

**Device list** (pick; name it; do not hide behind "subtle"):

| Device | What it is | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| Deflection | Answers about something smaller than the feeling | Asked about leaving, talks about the tea |
| Wrong answer | Answers a different question, revealing the want | "Açık mı, demli mi?" → "Az kaldı." |
| Ritual | A known everyday ritual says it (tea, greetings, a spoon across the glass) | Damla lays the spoon across the glass |
| Object hand-off | Giving or keeping a thing says the feeling | A feather for a pen |
| Repetition with change | A habit from an earlier scene, altered once | She stops saying "az kaldı" |
| Silence or a wordless beat | The line is cut and the action stays | She does not drink it |
| Body | The feeling leaks through the body (cheeks, quills, fluff) | Cheeks empty when he is listening |
| Misheard / mistaken | A small mistake and its correction | "...Musluk değilmiş." (rationed: overused in draft 0) |

## 1. Idea

List 5 to 8 seeds in one line each. At least two must change the
situation, not just the wording (different place, time, who is
present, what is in their hands). Pick two seeds with different
devices. Ideas are the AI's strength: be generous here and nowhere else.

## 2. Beat

For each seed, 4 to 7 beats. A beat is an action, an object or a tactic,
never a line: "Mochi offers tea; Damla answers the wrong question; the
glass stops at half; spoon across the glass." If the beats do not show
the change without any dialogue, the seed is weak.

## 3. Draft

Write lines into the beats. Speaking register, Turkish first, under the
cap. Stage directions in Turkish, short, present tense; they become
animation and sound, so they may say what the player will see, never what
anyone feels.

## 4. Subtext pass

Go line by line. For each line ask:

1. Does it **state a feeling**? Replace with behaviour or an object, or cut.
2. Does it **explain** the lore, the metaphor or the joke? Cut.
3. Does it **name the theme** (enough, quiet, home, together)? Cut or
   move it into an object.
4. Is it the **forbidden line** in disguise (a synonym, another
   character saying it, a narrator saying it)? Cut.
5. Does every line do a **tactic** to the other speaker? If it only
   informs, cut it or make it act.

Record each change as `before → after (reason)`.

## 5. Cut 30 % (full mode; in light mode, just trim)

Count spoken words and stage-direction words. Remove at least 30 % of
the total, deleting before rephrasing: whole lines first, then stage
directions the art will show anyway, then adjectives, then particles
that pad ("ama", "işte", "yani" unless they are the voice). Record the
counts. Professional editors of AI text mostly delete; insertions are
rare (LAMP, CHI 2025).

## 6. Read-aloud pass

Say every line at speaking speed. Mark:

- a stumble or a breath in the wrong place;
- written-only Turkish (textbook word order, "-ebilmek" politeness,
  "bu," as a pronoun, a calque);
- vowel harmony or suffix you had to think about;
- a line nobody would say to a friend's face.

Fix only the marked lines. The agent's read-aloud is a pre-check; the
real one is a person who did not write it, recorded as a voice note.

## 7. Critic

The critic only names what is **false**: a line a person would not say,
an emotion stated, an explanation, a tic from the banned list, a
generic cozy line that could be in another game, a device repeated, a
voice that survives the tag swap, a below-the-waterline leak. Format:

```
FALSE: "<exact quote>" — <why, in one clause>
```

No praise, no suggestions, no scores, no "consider". If nothing is
false, the critic writes `NOTHING FALSE FOUND` and one sentence on what
it checked. Up to five items; the most damning first.

## 8. Fix and package

Fix only what the critic named (cutting is a valid fix). Then build the
panel block. Variants are shuffled and shown as X / Y / Z with the old
draft included as one of them when one exists; the key sits in a
separate section that the founder removes before sending.

```
### Panel: <scene>

Context for readers (one line, no spoilers): <where, who>

**X**
<scene>

**Y**
<scene>

**Z**
<scene>

Questions: Which one sounds like people? Which line would you quote to a
friend? Did any line make you stumble? Did you feel something nobody
said? (Answer per letter.)
```

Key (do not send): `X = …, Y = …, Z = …`.

## Feedback (optional)

The lead agent is the writer and signs off the final line. The founder
and friends are readers: a cold read-aloud voice note, a blind
comparison, one real detail they offer. Their feedback informs the
writer; it is not a gate. When a reader's version of a line is simply
better, take it.
