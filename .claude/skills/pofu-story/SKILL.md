# Pofu story: write it well, then edit lightly

**Who writes.** The lead agent is the writer and owns the final line.
The founder and his friends give opinions; they are readers, not a gate
(founder, 2026-11-03). Write so that their notes are about taste, not
about obvious AI tells.

**What good means.** Natural, genuinely good narrative first: a voice
you can hear, rhythm that changes, concrete detail, characters who sound
like people, a little humour from the body and the everyday. Subtext,
cutting and the read-aloud are **editorial checks after the writing**,
not a mold to squeeze every line into. A line that says the thing
plainly is fine when a person would say it plainly.

Research behind this: [ai-story-methods.md](../../../docs/research/ai-story-methods.md).
First test: [story-tests/round-1.md](../../../docs/product/story-tests/round-1.md).

## Before you write

1. The source: [lore-bible.md](../../../docs/product/lore-bible.md)
   (premise, each resident's want, need, wound and tell, tone rules §6)
   and, for an island, [island-curation.md](../../../docs/product/island-curation.md).
2. [references/style-anchors.md](references/style-anchors.md). Read a few
   aloud in your head and take the **moves**, never the words.
3. The last scene written for the same characters: pick up a habit to
   echo, and do not reuse its main trick.

## The default path

1. **Know the scene.** In two or three lines, for yourself: what changes
   between the first beat and the last, what each speaker wants right
   now, and the one object in their hands. Know the thing nobody will
   say out loud.
2. **Write it like an author.** Turkish first, spoken register, at
   speaking speed in your head. Let the characters talk the way the
   people they are modelled on talk (an aunt at a çay ocağı, a kid on
   the ferry). Use real everyday ritual where it fits ("Kolay gelsin",
   a teaspoon across the glass). Let one line breathe; not every line
   is two words. Stage directions are short and show what the player
   will see and hear.
3. **Edit lightly, in three quick checks** (details in
   [references/revision-protocol.md](references/revision-protocol.md)):
   - _Subtext check:_ is anyone naming their own feeling, explaining
     the lore, or explaining a joke after making it? Fix those lines;
     leave the rest alone.
   - _Trim:_ cut what the art will show anyway and any line that only
     informs. Aim for shorter, not for a quota.
   - _Read aloud:_ fix stumbles, textbook Turkish, calques.
4. **Scan the tics** in [references/banned-tics.md](references/banned-tics.md)
   and the [subtext checklist](references/subtext-checklist.md). They are
   prompts to look, not rules to satisfy.
5. **Optional critic.** For a key beat (a stage 5, a myth line, Pofu's
   origin), run a critic in a fresh context (a sub-agent with only the
   scene and the checklists) that only quotes what rings false. Fix what
   you agree with.

**When stuck** or when a scene keeps coming out flat, use the full
protocol (card → ideas → beats → draft → subtext → cut 30 % → read-aloud
→ critic) in the same file. It is a tool for getting unstuck, not the
default; round 1 found it makes lines terse when used on everything.

## Variants and feedback

When the founder wants a comparison, write two variants that differ in
approach (a situation, a device, who speaks), not in wording, and add the
old draft if one exists. Package them blind with the panel block in the
protocol. Reader feedback is **optional input**: use it to learn, then
decide as the writer.

## Hard rules (these are product rules, not style)

- **Never in the world:** phones, screens, feeds, apps, "digital",
  "detox", "mindful", health claims, seeds as a price, a villain,
  anyone's death. Pofu never lectures and never guilts the user.
- **Below-the-waterline facts never appear in a line** (lore bible §0).
- **Endings are quiet and complete** (lore bible §6.8): no hook that
  pulls the player back, no moral.
- **Turkish first, natively:** whole lines, no spliced suffixes, no
  calques. English is a gloss here; release English is transcreated
  later by a native writer.
- Mark output `draft:ai` until the lead agent signs it off as final.

## Output

For each scene: the scene, an English gloss, and two or three lines of
honest notes (what you were going for, what you are unsure about). Keep
traces short; nobody reads them.
