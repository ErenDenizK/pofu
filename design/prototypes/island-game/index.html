What this proves

  • Seeds are world material. They gather while you are away (and a little per skip) and build the island. They never buy screen time.
  • Care, not price. Opening costs calm and Pofu's wellbeing. A quick check (3 min) only halves the calm, and Pofu is not jealous.
  • The breathing break. Past 60 minutes a day, every open waits for one to three breaths with Pofu. Never longer.
  • An archipelago. New islands drift close over time, each with its own small loop and neighbor.
  • Nothing dies. Unwell Pofu curls up; a quiet hour and a night's sleep bring him back.
