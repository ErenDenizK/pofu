What this proves

  • Care over currency. Opening an app costs Pofu's wellbeing and your built-up calm, never coins.
  • Played while away. Residents produce goodies at the calm multiplier; you collect on open.
  • Pre-planned growth. Fixed slots, five designed levels each; you choose the order (and a style at some levels).
  • Short, finished sessions. Collect, maybe upgrade, then “All caught up”.
  • Nothing dies. Unwell Pofu curls up; a quiet hour brings him back.
