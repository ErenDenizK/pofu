rule set

World: painted

  • ✓Pofu (44 pt and up)
  • ✓Scenes and props
  • ✓Badges, one medallion family
  • ✓Empty states, onboarding
  • ✓App icon, App Store art

Frame: native

  • ✕Buttons, toggles, menus
  • ✕Navigation, sheets, top bar
  • ✕Any text inside an image
  • ✕Chrome icons (SF Symbols)
  • ✕Pofu below 44 pt (vector glyph)

The image-dump test

World, not chrome? No other star on the screen? Variants derivable in code? No text? Listed in the prompt library? Five yeses, or it is not generated.
